Design an aircraft the way an aircraft company does

Every design is a whole light aircraft: the wing's airfoil (6 shape parameters, from OpenFOAM through the graph network), its area, aspect ratio, taper, sweep, twist and position, with tails sized to match. Each one is flown in 3D (vortex lattice, trimmed) and checked like a real design review:

Fastertop speed at full power at cruise altitude
Less impactkg of CO₂ per 100 km at cruise speed (fuel burnt × 3.1)
Lands slowerstall speed of the clean wing: shorter runways, gentler landings (and the CS-23 limit, 61 kt)
Must flystatic margin, stall starting inboard (not at the tips), trim, spar depth, span; a design that misses one is out
Can be trustedfive networks vote; the graph network gives the flow field as a second opinion; OpenFOAM verifies

The reference is the Cessna 172's own airfoil, the NACA 2412, on the reference wing.
