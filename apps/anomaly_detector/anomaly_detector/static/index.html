Every detector is calibrated only on the window you declare normal. With a label column, precision and recall are computed against it.

Benchmark on labelled data with known faults

Each run: two fault-free hours to calibrate, then a leak, a heater loss, a temperature-sensor bias, a level-sensor drift, a stuck flow meter and a partially closed drain valve, separated by normal periods. Seeds 101-103 were not used while developing the detectors. The public BATADAL adapter (PR #731) is not merged yet, so the benchmark is synthetic with exact ground truth.
