One form, data from four places

Engineering forms collect data that lives in other documents. Before a manufacturer can design a pressure vessel under ASME Section VIII Division 1, for example, the user's design requirements go on Form U-DR-1: service, design conditions, MDMT, corrosion allowances, loadings, materials, nozzles, joints and examination. Those answers are spread over the process datasheet, the mechanical datasheet and the owner's specifications, and compiling them by hand takes hours and misses things.

  1. Read every document: tables and "label: value" lines, with units.
  2. Compile every item of the form. Each value keeps its document, page and the exact text it came from.
  3. Flag conflicts between documents and the required items nobody states, so you know whom to ask.
  4. Fill the fillable PDF. Date, user and signature stay for the engineer.

ASME Form U-DR-1 is built in. Any other fillable PDF works too: its fields become the items. Optionally, a local LLM served by Ollama reads what the label rules miss; documents stay on your network.
