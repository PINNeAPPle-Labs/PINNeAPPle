One form, data from four places

Before a manufacturer can design a pressure vessel under ASME Section VIII Division 1, the user's design requirements go on Form U-DR-1: service, operating and design conditions, MDMT, corrosion allowances, loadings, materials, nozzles, joint types and examination. Those answers are spread over the process datasheet, the mechanical datasheet and the owner's specifications, and compiling them by hand takes hours and misses things.

  1. Read every document: tables and "label: value" lines, with units.
  2. Compile the 129 items of the form. Each value keeps its document, page and the exact text it came from.
  3. Flag conflicts between documents and the required items nobody states, so you know whom to ask.
  4. Fill the official fillable PDF. Date, user and signature stay for the engineer.
