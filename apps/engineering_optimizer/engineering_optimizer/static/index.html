Black-box design optimisation you can check

Pick the design variables, objectives and limits; the optimiser searches with a fast model and the chosen candidates are re-evaluated with the reference model or your solver.

MethodsNSGA-II (library and pymoo), SciPy differential evolution, Bayesian optimisation, a Latin-hypercube baseline
Evaluation modela pre-trained surrogate (pp.zoo), a library physics model, a model trained on your data, or an operator-configured solver
Feasibilitywhich constraints are active, the margin of each one, physical and manufacturing
Confirmationsurrogate vs reference at the candidates: the error and whether each one still meets the limits

Outputs are open formats: ParameterSpace, ModelRef and StudyResult JSON, CSV tables. The Benchmark Arena helps choose the model; pass its ModelRef here.
