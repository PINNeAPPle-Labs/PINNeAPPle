Physics Design Optimizer
Gradient, evolutionary and Bayesian optimisation of physical designs, compared on the same budget

Design problem

Six geometry parameters (base, fin count, fin thickness and height). Physics: a neural surrogate of the 3-D finite-volume heat-sink engine, differentiable end to end; the engine itself verifies.

Objectives (minimise)
Operating point
Limits

2nd-order CST section (6 shape weights) and the angle of attack, at Re 4e6. Minimise drag, maximise spar depth (20–35 % chord). Physics: an MLP ensemble trained on OpenFOAM k-ω SST runs.

Methods to compare

Which method finds the better design, and at what cost?

Every method gets the same model, the same number of evaluations and the same seeds. The study reports which ones converged, how close each got to the best front, and what it cost.
