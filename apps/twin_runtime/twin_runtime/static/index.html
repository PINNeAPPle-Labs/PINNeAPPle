Live twin of tank-1

A heated buffer tank (level and temperature, pump and heater). The built-in plant publishes its sensors over MQTT through the embedded broker; the twin subscribes and assimilates every reading.

Inject a fault in the simulated plant

Simulation and monitoring only: the twin sends no command to any equipment.

What the twin keeps up to date

Estimated statelevel and temperature with their spread, from an ensemble Kalman filter; a leak flow and the heater efficiency as estimated parameters
Forecastthe next 10 minutes with a 5-95 % band, including the uncertainty of the held inputs
Alertslimits, a forecast reaching a limit, parameters out of range, data disagreeing with the model
Validitydrift checks on the model's own predictions; a revalidation request when conditions leave its training range

MQTT

Publish JSON readings to pinneapple/<asset>/sensors. With the embedded broker, publish port 1883 of the container (TWIN_MQTT_HOST=0.0.0.0, TWIN_MQTT_PORT=1883) or point TWIN_MQTT_URL at your own broker (Mosquitto, EMQX, HiveMQ).

Python (PINNeAPPle)

Built on PINNeAPPle (pinneapple_systems.operations: lumped tank model, operator inference with inputs, EnKF twin runtime, drift monitoring with assess_drift · pinneapple_data.contracts · embedded MQTT broker). Monetization: per monitored asset, data volume and inferences.
