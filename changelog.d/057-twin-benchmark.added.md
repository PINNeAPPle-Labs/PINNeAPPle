The twin benchmark (#57): `pinneapple_systems.digital_twin.benchmark` (`twin_benchmark`, `list_benchmark`, `cases`, `benchmark_case`) measures a twin on one case with a written protocol and a results table -- state error against truth and against persistence, the per-reading latency against the data period and the recalibration against the window it collects, robustness to the plant being noisier than the twin was told, four sensor-failure modes (dropout, outage, stuck, bias), anomaly detection rate and false alarms, remaining useful life from the twin's own health indicator with a measured statement when it cannot be predicted, and the decision the estimate drives, priced by a stated cost matrix. The heated channel is the default case because sensor failure needs two outputs.
