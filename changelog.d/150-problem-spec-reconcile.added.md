- The three problem specifications are reconciled, with the losses written down (`pinneapple_physics.pde_environment.reconcile`, decision record in `docs/strategy/D1_problem_specs.md`, issue #150): `pde_to_design` turns a PDE `ProblemSpec` into the elicited description a report or the assistant shows, `design_to_pde` turns it back and **requires** the `pde_kind` — which equation is meant is the decision the conversation does not make, so the converter refuses to invent it — and the new `upd_condition_to_pde` / `pde_condition_to_upd` bridge a sampling condition (`pinneapple_data.upd_types`, one UPD shard condition) to and from a PDE condition, returning a `ConditionBridge` that carries what the PDE side cannot hold (the equation string, the point count) and lists what the conversion could not do (a value function cannot be recovered from a string). No new class, as D1 asks: `SHARED_FIELDS` is the field-by-field table the decision record prints and the tests read, and `LOSSES` states in one sentence per direction what is dropped and why that is right. Round trips are tested in both directions for every shared field (name, fields, coordinates, bounds, equation kind, units), and the tests assert the loss rather than hiding it.
