- `pinneapple_analysis.condition` is the conditioning layer behind `pp.condition` (issue #206, roadmap X22): it decides the scales and the loss weights of a problem *before* training starts, from the numbers of the problem rather than from a guess. `condition_report(coordinates=, fields=, residual_terms=, parameters=)` returns one `Scale` per variable (centre and half-width, so `apply` sends the declared range to `[-1, 1]` and `invert` brings it back), one weight per residual term (`(target / magnitude)^2`, so every weighted term is O(target)), and the dimensionless groups the problem admits — and it says which of them it could **not** build and what each one needed, instead of leaving them out or defaulting them. `dimensionless_groups` reports `Re`, `Pe`, `Ma`, `Pr`, `Fr`, `Kn`, `Gr`, `Ra`, `Ri`, `Bi` from SI parameters through `pinneapple_physics.physics_audit.regime`, so a statement of density, velocity, length and viscosity answers `Re = 20 000` directly; two viscosities that contradict each other (`kinematic_viscosity` against `dynamic_viscosity / density`) are refused rather than silently preferred. Measured: on a 0.1 m slab with `k = 0.15 W/(m K)` and `f = 3000 W/m^3` the two loss terms of the raw problem differ by exactly 900 (the square of `f` against the temperature rise) and the report levels them to 1; the weight for that term is `(1/3000)^2`. `scaling_benchmark` is the claim of the issue measured rather than asserted: the same 1 x 32 x 32 x 1 network, the same seed, optimiser, budget (600 Adam steps) and loss structure, trained on the same problem three ways — raw physical units, the hand-tuned rescaling a person does, and the automatic report — gives first-below-5 %-error at step 260 (hand) and 91 (auto) on seed 0, 375 and 151 on seed 1, 316 and 77 on seed 2, with the raw arm never reaching the tolerance in any of them; final relative errors against the analytic `ambient + f x (L - x) / (2 k)` are 1.6e-3 (hand) and 1.7e-3 (auto) on seed 0. 13 tests in `tests/pinneapple_analysis/test_condition.py`. `pinneapple_flow.coverage` carries the reason the layer is not itself a flow node.
