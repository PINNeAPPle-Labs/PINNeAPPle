- `pinneapple_analysis.numerics.regimes` diagnoses which regime a problem is in and which strategy answers it (issue #207, roadmap X23) — as a rule table over measured numbers, not a model. `diagnose_ode` samples the stiffness ratio and the Jacobian spectrum along the coarse implicit trajectory `stiffness_report` already runs, and reports `stiff` (ratio at or above 1e3) or `oscillatory` (largest `|Im λ|/|λ|` at or above 0.5). `diagnose_field` measures a field on a line — the gradient concentration against the field's typical slope, the layer as the full width at half maximum of the gradient, where that layer sits, the jump from one end of the line to the other, the turning points and the two-band structure of the spectrum — and reports `boundary_layer`, `shock`, `sharp_feature`, `high_frequency` or `multiscale`. Each regime carries the strategies that answer it (implicit solver, adaptive time stepping and time marching for `stiff`; adaptive sampling, coordinate scaling, curriculum and XPINN for a layer; adaptive sampling, causal training, viscosity continuation and XPINN for a shock; Fourier features for an oscillation; a curriculum for two scales), each with the reason it is the remedy, and a report only lists the strategies for the regimes it found. Measured: Robertson's kinetics gives a stiffness ratio above 1e8 and reports `stiff`; `1 - exp(-x/0.01)` gives a thickness of 0.0070 against the analytic `δ ln 2 = 0.00693` and reports `boundary_layer`; `tanh((x - 1/2)/0.01)` gives 0.0170 against `2 δ atanh(1/√2) = 0.01763`, an interior location and a full jump, and reports `shock`. A cubic, a layer a third of the domain thick, a wide bump and a constant field all report `smooth` with no strategy, and a thin interior bump is reported as `sharp_feature` rather than as a shock because the field comes back. A layer with fewer than three points across it says so (`REGIME.UNRESOLVED`) instead of reporting a thickness that is the grid. 23 tests in `tests/pinneapple_analysis/test_numerics_regimes.py`. `stiffness_report` now also returns the sampled `states`, so the spectrum needs no second integration.
