Decision report from an ensemble (#219, roadmap S38): `pinneapple_analysis.decision.decision_report` takes the draws of an uncertainty ensemble, the quantity of interest and the hard constraints written as `pp.Constraint` (#221), and returns the page a decision is taken on -- the distribution of the quantity of interest (mean, deviation, quantiles, extremes), the **probability of violating each hard constraint** (the fraction of realizations the constraint engine rejected, not a second statistic computed elsewhere, with the soft ones kept apart), **which input combinations drive the risk** (the cells of the input space cut at the median of the two most influential variables, each with its size and its violation rate, plus the per-variable threshold beyond which no violation was observed and the correlation that justifies it), the **cases that drive the risk** (the realizations with the largest violation), and a **recommendation with its validity domain** (#58) and the trust status of the evidence (#S8) -- an accuracy check against the reference, the envelope containment, the size of the sample and the spread, so a probability measured on a model nobody validated does not read the same as one that was. `DecisionReport.to_markdown()` is the printable page; `to_dict()` is the same content for a pipeline. Measured on the `inverse_heat` plate benchmark (200 realizations, coarse 40x24 finite-volume model against the 200x120 grid as reference): `T_max` = 66.87 +- 36.14 degC, `P(T_max <= 85 degC) = 0.180`, `P(T_max <= 60 degC) = 0.435`, the risk concentrated in "h <= 56 W/m2K and P > 12.1 W" (52 cases, 100 % violation of the 60 degC limit), and the coarse-versus-fine disagreement at 0.008 % relative.
