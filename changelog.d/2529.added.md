- `FNO2d` takes five optional arguments that reproduce PhysicsNeMo's `FNO` exactly — `lift_hidden` (the two-step
  lifting), `final_activation` (the last Fourier block left linear), `decoder_hidden`/`decoder_activation` (a
  pointwise MLP head) and `spectral_init` (Li et al.'s uniform spectral weights) — so a model ported from
  PhysicsNeMo can be rebuilt, and its state dict transplanted, on this side. Their defaults reproduce the previous
  architecture and `state_dict` names, so existing checkpoints load unchanged.
- `pinneapple_neural.physicsnemo_bridge.fno_compare` measures the parity instead of assuming it: the weight map
  between `physicsnemo.models.fno.FNO` and `FNO2d`, the transplant (which mirrors PhysicsNeMo's frozen lifting
  biases), the relative L2 of the forward pass and of the gradients on the same weights, and a same-protocol trained
  comparison against the exact advection-diffusion propagator, at the base resolution and on resolution transfer.
  `python -m benchmarks.fno_physicsnemo_parity` writes the numbers; `docs/dev/PHYSICSNEMO_PARITY_FNO.md` reads them
  (#2529).
