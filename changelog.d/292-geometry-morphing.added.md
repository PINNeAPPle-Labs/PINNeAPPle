- **Geometry morphing for design spaces (#292):** `pp.geom.morph` deforms an arbitrary surface mesh with the topology unchanged, so a field stays comparable vertex by vertex — `FFD` (a Bernstein control lattice; the design variables are control-point displacements, with a symmetry plane and keep-out regions resolved into the parameter vector, 2.1 ms per design on the 21 760-triangle road car of `data/geometries`), `RBFMorph` (handles on chosen surface points, a thin-plate-spline field with a linear part that reproduces a rigid motion to 4e-16 m, and points held fixed), and `ShapeModes` (PCA of a registered family, with `project`/`residual` to measure what the kept modes cannot express). `DesignSpace` puts bounds, keep-out boxes, Latin-hypercube sampling and a validity gate in front of any of the three, and records every refusal with its reason and number. The gate takes watertightness, orientation, degenerate faces and enclosed volume from `pinneapple_data.cae.quality`, and adds what the CAE layer does not have: new self-intersecting face pairs (grid + AABB + Möller-Trumbore, and only the pairs that did not already intersect in the reference), faces turned inside out, volume and frontal-area bands, and an optional wall-thickness ray probe. Guide: `docs/guides/geometry_morphing.md`; example: `examples/geometry/09_surface_morphing_design_space.py`.
