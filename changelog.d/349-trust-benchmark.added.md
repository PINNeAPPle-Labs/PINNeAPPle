- `pinneapple_tools.benchmark_suite.trust`: the trust benchmark of #349 -- one protocol that runs four injected
  failure kinds (a hidden regime change, a sensor fault, a slow drift and an out-of-domain reading, each with the
  reading where it begins as ground truth) through every candidate model, watched by the same calibrated monitor
  (`pinneapple_analysis.trust.drift`), and ranks them on what they did about it: missed cases, readings to detect,
  false alarms per 1000, the error after the change and the coverage of a stated band. `run_suite` is the one entry
  point; `benchmarks/trust/results.json` and `docs/trust_benchmark.md` are generated by
  `python scripts/trust_benchmark.py --run` and refused by `--check` when either drifted from the measurements.
