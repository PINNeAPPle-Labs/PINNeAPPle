- `pinneapple_analysis.inverse_problems.gp_symbolic`: **symbolic regression by genetic programming, with the physics checked before an expression is reported, and with SINDy measured beside it on the same target.** `Expression` is the tree (its text round-trips through `pinneapple_data.expressions`, its dimension comes from `pinneapple_data.physical_units`); `PhysicsConstraints` is the filter — dimensional signature with the unit of the prefactor, sign, the limit where the term has to vanish, an even/odd symmetry and a balance the term's integral has to add up to; `GeneticProgramming` is the search (subtree crossover and mutation, constants by linear scaling, NSGA-II survival over error against complexity, dimensionally inconsistent children never bred); `discover_expression` runs it; `compare_gp_and_sindy` runs the same columns and the same target through `CandidateLibrary` + `SINDyIdentifier` and returns both arms' error, term count and seconds. Measured on a periodic 1-D advection–diffusion–reaction field (`advection_diffusion_reaction_case`, its solver held to the library's exact advection–diffusion solution to 2.4e-7 of its size): on a **quadratic sink the degree-2 library holds**, both arms reach the finite-difference floor of 0.17 % and SINDy costs 1/1200 of the search; on a **cubic sink the degree-2 library cannot write**, SINDy fits 2.07 % with the wrong form over three terms while the search writes `-0.7006 u^3` at 0.18 % in four nodes — and with a degree-3 library SINDy finds it again and is a thousand times cheaper; on **`-0.8 sqrt(u)`** the search finds it at 0.00 % and no polynomial library does (degree 6 imitates it to 0.11 % inside the sampled range and is not the law). The filter refuses a source where the physics is a sink, a cubic rate against a quadratic prefactor, `log(u)` and `sin(u)` of a concentration, `u^2 + u`, and an integral 3 % off the conservation statement — each with the reason in words. Noise: recovery stays at 100 % for the search from 0 to 50 % noise on the target, while degree-2 SINDy falls to 80 % at 10 % and 0 % at 50 % (5 seeds per level, `noise_sweep`). What it does not do yet: one prefactor per expression (a multi-term law still needs SINDy), and no PySR back end.
