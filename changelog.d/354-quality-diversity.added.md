The quality-diversity search (#354): `pinneapple_design.design_optimizer.quality_diversity` keeps the best design in every cell of a behaviour space (MAP-Elites over a grid: Gaussian and line mutations, Latin-hypercube first batch, immigrant share, parallel batch evaluation), reports whether the archive is a map and not a point (`diversity`, `assert_diverse`, with fill, design spread and the largest cluster), and points the same archive at a model instead of at a design: `find_failure_region` uses fitness = the model's own error, refuses the cases the reference physics cannot answer instead of archiving them, and returns the failing cells with the number, the design and the behaviour interval that produced it -- the trust layer's out-of-domain region found by search. Behaviour axes are outcomes, from the reference physics or from `pinneapple_design.qualitative.describe` (`descriptor_vector`, `descriptor_axes`), never from the model under test. `examples/quality_diversity` is the airfoil-free demo on the plate-fin heat sink: at 600 evaluations the failure search finds a 66.4 % surrogate error against 57.7 % for random sampling (all five seeds) and 62.6 % for Latin hypercube (three of five), and the design map is committed as an image in `docs/guides/quality_diversity.md`.
