The three Ashton automotive CFD datasets (AhmedML 500 Ahmed bodies, DrivAerML 484 public DrivAer variants, WindsorML 350 Windsor run folders), all CC BY-SA 4.0 and all in the same on-disk layout, behind one reader in `pinneapple_data.ashton`: a catalogue carrying each dataset's paper, licence, credit, reference quantities and the source of every reference number; `open_dataset`/`case`/`measured_forces`/`volume_subsample` reading the surface field (VTK XML `.vtp` and `.vtu`, inline binary, only the named arrays decoded), the published quadrature, the force CSV and the design parameters, with a missing case raising a `CaseNotPresent` that names the file, its URL, its size and the `fetch` call; a byte-range volume subsample that returns exactly the elements a full read would have; and `case_bundle` carrying licence and citation in the bundle's provenance.

The benchmark harness (`pinneapple_data.ashton.benchmark`) puts published baselines and measurements in one table: the AB-UPT surface-pressure numbers for AhmedML and DrivAerML (L2 0.0272 and 0.0388) and the dataset papers' own solver-versus-tunnel force coefficients (Cd 0.285/0.299, 0.274/0.255, 0.33/0.33) are *declared*, quoted with table and scale; the measured column fills itself from the data and from a model, and says `awaiting_data` (with the size) when neither is there. Two things are stated rather than rounded off: the AB-UPT paper reports AhmedML and DrivAerML only, so WindsorML has no surrogate baseline and its row says so; and the two pressure columns are on different scales (Cp against Pa), so only L2 is comparable. The baseline that runs offline is `constant_field_baseline`, the area-weighted mean field, which the fixture scores at L2 0.352.

The cross-family experiment (`pinneapple_data.ashton.trust`) trains on one body family and walks the parameter box towards the other in six graded levels, measuring three trust signals: the Mahalanobis embedding distance through `TrustGate` with its threshold calibrated on a held-out in-family split, the share of cases flagged, and the coverage of a 90 % split-conformal interval on Cd. On the synthetic fixture (seed 0, 36 training and 12 calibration cases) the label-free alarm crosses before the drag error doubles in both directions -- flagged at t = 0.4 against the error at t = 0.6 training on the rounded family, and t = 0.2 against 0.6 in the reverse -- the embedding distance rises 2.89 to 38.18 and 2.90 to 16.59, and the conformal coverage collapses from 100 %/90 % in-family to 0 % on the other family. The same run shows the Cp *distribution* barely moving (0.026-0.044 relative L2 at every level) while the Cd error rises 5-10x, so the ordering is claimed on the coefficient and the flat column is reported as flat; the module also reports where the ordering is not established (a coarser fixture mesh, and one seed whose in-family fit was so good that "twice the in-family error" became a strict threshold).

Offline only, and stated as such: nothing downloads unless asked, the fixture (`pinneapple_data.ashton.fixture`) writes the synthetic dataset the tests read -- including a 2 x 1 x 1 box whose drag is 1.4 on paper and 1.4 to 1e-9 through the reader, and a case whose CSV is integrated by triangle decomposition so the comparison with the loader's polygon integral checks the integral rather than the same arithmetic twice -- and no number is claimed for the 41 TB. See `docs/guides/ashton_datasets.md` for the licence, the per-dataset credit and the split between what was measured and what needs the data.
