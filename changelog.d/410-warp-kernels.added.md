- Warp kernels for the accretion solver (#410): `RIAFConfig(backend="warp")` runs `pinneapple_physics.blackhole` as NVIDIA Warp kernels in `hydro_warp` -- one fused kernel per SSP-RK2 stage (MUSCL-minmod reconstruction, HLL fluxes, the two azimuthal viscous stresses, the geometric sources and the update), one for the mass and angular-momentum flux through `r_in`/`r_out`, and one for conserved-to-primitive plus the ghost cells -- with the PyTorch solver unchanged as the reference. Measured on an Apple Silicon CPU with one torch thread: 4.09 vs 4.14 ms per step on 64 x 32, 6.08 vs 8.82 on 128 x 64 and 23.87 vs 32.97 on 256 x 128 (1.4x, from 0.73 against 1.01 us per cell), the primitives agreeing to 3e-14 relative and the budgets to 2e-15; the GPU columns are reported as not measured, because this machine has neither a CUDA device nor a CUDA build of `warp-lang`. `hydro.accreted_mass` keeps the reference's step differentiable (out of place, fixed `dt`) and `hydro_warp.tape_accreted_mass` does the same through `wp.Tape`: both give d(mass accreted)/d(alpha) = 6.051951e-11 against 6.051962e-11 from a finite difference, 1.9e-6 relative. `warp-lang` stays optional -- `hydro_warp.warp_status()` names what is missing and the install line, and only a request for the backend raises. `hydro_warp.WARP_PORTS` records that `compressible_fv` and `shallow_water_fv` are not ported, with the reason and the seam they will plug into.
