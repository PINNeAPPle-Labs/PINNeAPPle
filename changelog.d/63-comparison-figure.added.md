The standard field comparison in the benchmark report (#63): one figure per case with a planar field -- reference, surrogate and the absolute difference side by side, the two solutions on one shared colour scale and the L2 error quoted in the number -- drawn with `pp.viz.compare_solutions` and measured with `pp.viz.field_error_metrics`, so a figure and a table cannot disagree. The surrogate is the case's best field model on the metric that judges it, from its full-budget seed-0 run; the trainers store the two arrays as `runs/<job>.npz`, the same sidecar the problems' own campaigns write. The report's new **Field comparison** section says how many cases have the figure, and for `burgers` (a 1-D profile) and the two CalculiX parts (a 3-D node cloud) prints the reason declared in `Problem.comparison_absent` instead of a picture that means nothing.
