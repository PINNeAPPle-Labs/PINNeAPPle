- `pp.viz.turntable(field, "case.gif")` turns a stack of 2-D fields into a clip in one call: the camera sweeps a full turn while the field runs from the first instant to the last, at 1080p by default, with a caption naming the field, its unit and the instant, a colour bar and a discreet mark in the corner. `.gif` needs only Pillow and `.mp4` the `ffmpeg` binary, both drawn on matplotlib's Agg canvas, so it needs no display, no GPU and no pyvista. `frames=` makes a smooth orbit out of a handful of solved instants, `clim` and `deform` control the scale and the relief, and with no path it returns the frames as arrays. The sweep stops one step short of closing the turn so a looping GIF does not stutter (issue #64).
