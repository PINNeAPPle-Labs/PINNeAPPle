- **Behavior:** `pp.viz.field_view` refuses a field that declares no `unit`, naming the field and saying what to pass. A rendered field is a picture of colours until it says what it is in; `"unit": "-"` states that a field is dimensionless, which is a declaration and not an omission. Two internal callers passed no unit and now do: the Flow `field3d` node (which takes the solution record's unit, `"-"` when it has none) and the CalculiX `solver_view` (which says `model units`, because an `.frd` record does not carry one and inventing `MPa` would be a lie) (issue #67).
