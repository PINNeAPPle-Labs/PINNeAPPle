- `pp.viz.figure_caption(field, unit, ...)` composes the caption a figure has to carry: the field and its unit, the colour range in that unit, the exaggeration of a deformed shape (`deformation x25, lengths in mm`), the instant, and the method with its validation status — `von Mises [MPa], 0 to 512.4 MPa, deformation x25, lengths in m, FEM (validated)`. The unit has no default: `None` is refused, and `"-"` is how a dimensionless field declares itself. `to_dict()` gives the same facts as data, for a sidecar. `pp.viz.field_view` puts one caption per field in `meta["captions"]` (with the exaggeration factor the view will actually use) and the one on screen in `meta["caption"]`, and `pp.viz.turntable` now composes its caption with the same function instead of its own formatting (issue #67).
