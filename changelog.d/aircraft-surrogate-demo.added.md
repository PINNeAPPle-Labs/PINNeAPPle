`pinneapple_design.aero.aircraft_surrogate` and `aircraft_surrogate_demo`: a surrogate of a 3-D aircraft's surface pressure jump, trained on vortex-lattice data and graded by VeriPhysics. The demo starts from a request written in words (PT or EN, `parse_request`) and runs three rounds on it: a quick model that is rejected; a full model that is approved but rejects the request, because the request lies outside its design space; and a design space extended to the request, where the request is accepted. It also draws an acceptance map checked cell by cell against the solver and, when `bpy` is installed, renders the flow in Cycles with streamlines. The flow node is `aero.aircraft_surrogate`, with a capability that matches PT/EN requests. `vlm.loads_from_circulation` and `vlm.trefftz` give the solver's coefficients for any circulation (#1438).
