The aircraft surrogate study now delivers what it trained: `surrogate_model.pt` with a `model_card.json` (validated design space, VeriPhysics decision and checks, measured errors, cost, library version, limits), and the solver data as `dataset.npz` with a `data_card.json`. The demo ships every round's model, cards and figures. `load_surrogate(path)` loads a model back (`torch.load(weights_only=True)`) and predicts the ΔCp field with its spread, plus CL, CDi and Cm; outside the validated domain it refuses with the reasons (#1462).
