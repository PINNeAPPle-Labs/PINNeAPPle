Six base method workflows in the Flow Studio (#1185), each proposed by the planner and checked with measured numbers: **surrogate modeling** (`pinneapple_analysis.surrogate.workflow`: a DOE over a tool or a table, `recommend_architecture` decides (or none), the library's families on one protocol, checks on new tool calls, an export that refuses to extrapolate; #1186), **use a trained model** (`model_source`: zoo, exported, TorchScript, ONNX or callable models through an applicability gate — range, units, reference solves or the zoo's stored reference — forward or inverse, re-verified by the solver; #1190), **VeriPhysics grade of an external model** (`pinneapple_veriphysics.model_grade`: PDE, condition, reference, applicability and robustness checks by finite differences at the model's own precision, or on its predictions through a local RBF; #1187), **inverse modeling** (`inverse_problems.workflow`: identifiability before the fit, estimate with intervals, fit / held-out / bounds / white-residual / synthetic-recovery checks; #1189), **equation discovery** (`inverse_problems.discovery`: SINDy and PDE-FIND with held-out selection, bootstrap inclusion and intervals, and the discovered system simulated against data it never fitted; #1192) and **model benchmarking** (`surrogate.benchmark_workflow`: equal data and test points over seeds, paired-bootstrap intervals, a winner only when separated; #1191).
