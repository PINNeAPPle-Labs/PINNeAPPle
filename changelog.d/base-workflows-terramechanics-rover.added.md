- **Terramechanics** base workflow (#1199, part of #1185): `pinneapple_simulation.terramechanics.workflow.terramechanics_workflow` takes a database soil or identifies one from plate (bevameter) and shear-box tests through `inverse_workflow` (Bekker `k_c`, `k_phi`, `n` from at least two plate widths — one width is refused as not separable — and Mohr-Coulomb / Janosi `c`, `phi`, `K`), computes sinkage, drawbar pull and torque against slip with the Wong-Reece stresses (load balance, quadrature and monotonicity checks), designs the minimum-mass wheel (radius, width, grouser height) for a drawbar pull within slip, sinkage and torque limits, re-verifies it with the full model, asks `recommend_architecture` whether a surrogate pays off, and reproduces the published single-wheel tests of Ding et al. (2011): drawbar-pull RMSE 9.6 % / 5.0 % of the load (smooth / grousered), torque 4.6 % / 4.2 %, sinkage 24 % / 29 % of the largest measured. Flow node `terramechanics.workflow`, bundled flow `terramechanics_workflow.json`, planner capability in Portuguese and English.
- **Rover** base workflow (#1200, part of #1185): `pinneapple_simulation.vehicle.mission_design.rover_workflow` sizes the lightest rover for a planet, terrain and mission: grade capability and front-wheel step climbing with Wong-Reece wheels, tip-over angles, sinkage, drive power, a solar array over a sinusoidal day, a battery for the night and a survival heater (mass build-up to a fixed point); the mission traverse is then time-stepped with the battery state of charge, and the battery is grown by the depth of discharge the traverse measures (the night-only sizing misses the dawn and dusk deficits: 82.2 -> 90.8 W h on the default Mars mission). Sojourner's published array gives 13.9 W against the reported 16 W peak (-12.9 %, band ±25 %; the Mars sky transmission is a stated assumption). Flow node `rover.mission_design`, bundled flow `rover_mission_design.json`, planner capability in Portuguese and English.
