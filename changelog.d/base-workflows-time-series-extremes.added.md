Two more base workflows in the Flow Studio (#1185), each proposed by the planner and checked with measured numbers: **time-series prediction and modeling** (`pinneapple_systems.time_series.workflow`: missing values, Hampel outliers, seasonality, ADF / KPSS stationarity, permutation entropy and the Lyapunov time (Rosenstein with an automatic linear-region fit, new `fit="auto"`), leakage checks on covariates and folds; persistence / seasonal-naive / mean baselines, ARIMA with the order by BIC and additive Holt-Winters (`pinneapple_systems.time_series.statistical`, NumPy / SciPy only), gradient boosting, a TCN and a fitted ODE when the series has one, on one rolling-origin backtest with skill against the best baseline and split-conformal intervals with their measured coverage; node `ts.workflow`, flow `time_series_prediction`; #1197) and **extreme climate** (`pinneapple_analysis.extremes`: block maxima or peaks over a threshold with runs declustering and a threshold-stability rule, GEV / GPD by maximum likelihood with delta-method and profile-likelihood intervals, covariates in the GEV location and log-scale selected by likelihood ratio, return levels and periods, Gaussian / Gumbel copulas for compound events, a probability-ratio attribution with bootstrap intervals, QQ / return-level / Anderson-Darling diagnostics and a coverage study; the bundled Port Pirie, Fremantle and rain series reproduce the values printed in Coles (2001); node `climate.extremes`, flow `extreme_climate`; #1198).
