`solve_pde` trains the presets whose boundary condition is the tag `"boundary"` (`laplace_2d`, `poisson_2d`, `reaction_diffusion_2d`, the 3-D heat and wave presets, ten in all): on a `ProblemSpec` that tag is the boundary of its box, so its points are sampled on the spatial faces. They used to be refused (and before that, silently trained without a boundary condition). Every other tag (`"inlet"`, `"wall"`, ...) still needs real geometry.
