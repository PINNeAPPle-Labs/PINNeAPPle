- `pinneapple_simulation.pipelines.car_pressure`: a surface-pressure dataset generator over a parametric car family
  (three roof lines, open/closed wheel arches, thirteen numeric design variables) — the library's 3-D
  lattice-Boltzmann tunnel labels the wall faces of every design, and the campaign writes a field dataset plus the
  coefficients table and a manifest that records the resolution, the Reynolds number reachable there and every design
  that failed.
