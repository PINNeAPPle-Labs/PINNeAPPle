- **Experimental.** `globe_valve(..., solid=True)`: a globe valve as a real 3-D domain (rounded body, ports, a bonnet on a perpendicular axis, the seat ring and the plug) meshed hexa-dominantly with `snappyHexMesh`, because a body of revolution around the flow axis has no bonnet branch. Example in `examples/cfd_valvula_globo`, with the staged pressure ramp, the time mean and the numbers of the case (`globe_valve_experiment`). The Flow node `cfd.compressible_passage` gained `modo="transiente_3d"`, which runs that same function — the library and the app run one experiment, not two.
