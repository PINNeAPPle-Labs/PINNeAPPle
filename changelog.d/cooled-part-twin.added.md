`pinneapple_systems.digital_twin.cooled_part` and `pinneapple_flow.study_steps.twin_step`: a 3-D thermal digital twin of a part cooled by internal passages, from its geometry file. The part goes on a voxel grid with its coolant passages found. Transient conduction with convection per passage is reduced by POD-Galerkin, and an ensemble Kalman filter keeps the field and each passage's coefficient in step with the thermocouples. It alarms when a passage clogs, and writes a 3-D field view. Without live data, it is checked on a simulated replay from a finer-grid plant. The `twin` study kind now names this step, and verifies inside it (`verify.replay`).
