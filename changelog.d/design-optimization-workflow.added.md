- **Design optimization** base workflow in the Flow Studio (#1188, part of #1185): `pinneapple_design.design_optimizer.workflow.design_optimization` optimises the geometry dimensions and parameters of a library tool under constraints (SLSQP with finite differences on a mesh that moves with the geometry, NSGA-II for several objectives, a Gaussian-process surrogate refined where the optimiser looks when `recommend_architecture` says it pays off), re-solves the optimum on refined meshes (Richardson extrapolation, observed order, grid convergence index), corrects the coarse-mesh error and searches again until the correction settles, and checks convergence, mesh quality, every constraint on the verified values, KKT sensitivities (the objective's price of each active limit), robustness to manufacturing tolerances (with an optional back-off to 95 % feasibility) and the known optimum of a reference model. The bundled case `cantilever.tapered_cantilever` is a gmsh-parametrized tapered cantilever solved by plane-stress FEM, checked against beam theory (`tapered_cantilever_beam`): the minimum mass under 150 MPa and 8 mm is found within 0.04 % of the beam-theory optimum (7.850 kg), and `export_step` writes the STEP of the optimum and reads its volume back. Flow node `design.optimization`, bundled flow `design_optimization.json`, planner capability in Portuguese and English.
