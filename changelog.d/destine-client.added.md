`pinneapple_data.datasets.DestinEClient`: the Destination Earth (DestinE) platform, the EU programme behind the km-scale Climate DT. The catalogue is open and needs no account — 348 collections when checked (72 of them Climate DT: ICON, IFS-FESOM, IFS-NEMO, historical / SSP3-7.0 / T+2.0 K, generations 1 and 2), with each collection's variables and units, dimensions, temporal extent and licence (`CC-BY-4.0` for the Digital Twins). Items and asset downloads go through an OAuth2 `client_credentials` token (`DESTINE_CLIENT_ID` / `DESTINE_CLIENT_SECRET`), and a refusal is reported for what it is: 401 names the service-account key, 403 names the platform's upgraded access, which the Digital Twins collections need before their fields are served. `to_arrays` projects a search onto time and bounding boxes.
