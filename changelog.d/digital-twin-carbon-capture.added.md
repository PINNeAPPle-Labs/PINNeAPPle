`pinneapple_systems.digital_twin.library` gains a post-combustion carbon capture island: a 30 wt% MEA absorber-stripper behind a flue stack, asked the two questions a capture project is actually asked. Can the solvent carry the target? The carbon balance puts what the capture rate demands against what the circulating amine can carry between its lean loading and the loading it reaches equilibrium at, so an undersized circulation reports the gap instead of quietly capturing less. Can the steam pay for it? The energy balance puts the duty the declared stripping steam can deliver against the breakdown the stripper needs — desorption enthalpy, sensible heat of the whole circulation, and the water that leaves with the product. The specific reboiler duty falls out at 3.1 GJ per tonne for the default plant, inside the band a real MEA plant occupies, and the run states where its ideal-gas CO2 compressor stops being right, because above about 80 bar CO2 is a dense phase and the model overstates what the last stages cost (#1809).
