`pinneapple_systems.digital_twin.library`: eleven reference models — FPSO and subsea production, a blast furnace, a chemical synthesis loop, a reusable booster, a lunar base, a gas pipeline network, a compression station, a green hydrogen plant, a seawater desalination train, an industrial refrigeration plant and a data centre — each with declared-ranges SI parameters, a machine-readable result carrying two independent routes to every conservation law, invariants that must hold for any parameter set, and its own list of what it does not verify. The FPSO solves its operating point where inflow meets lift, the furnace gets its top gas and degree of direct reduction from the carbon and oxygen balances, the chemical loop's steady state is solved in closed form, the booster's boostback burn is solved so the stage lands on its pad, the lunar base is sized by the 354-hour night, the pipeline's throughput is the flow at which the pressure the line needs equals the pressure the supply offers, the compression station proves that the equal-ratio split is the cheapest one instead of asserting it, the electrolyser's hydrogen is Faraday's law and its heat the overpotential above the thermoneutral voltage, the desalination train derives its brine concentration from the salt balance before using it to set the osmotic pressure and checks every run against the reversible separation work, the ammonia plant builds its cycle from Antoine and Watson and reports the property model's own inconsistency instead of hiding it, and the data centre states that its single balance is a completeness check on the PUE roll-up. Fluids are ideal gases and solutions ideal; a rich gas, a brine above about 70 g/L and a data hall that needs CFD are outside these models. `catalogue()`, `run()`, `describe()` and `compare()` are the whole surface (#1770, #1772, #1774, #1777, #1779, #1784, #1801, #1807, #1813, #1814, #1819).
