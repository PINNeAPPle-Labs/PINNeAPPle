`pinneapple_systems.digital_twin.library`: five reference models — FPSO and subsea production, a blast furnace, a chemical synthesis loop, a reusable booster and a lunar base — each with declared-ranges SI parameters, a machine-readable result carrying two independent routes to every conservation law, invariants that must hold for any parameter set, and its own list of what it does not verify. The FPSO solves its operating point where inflow meets lift, the furnace gets its top gas and degree of direct reduction from the carbon and oxygen balances, the chemical loop's steady state is solved in closed form, the booster's boostback burn is solved so the stage lands on its pad, and the lunar base is sized by the 354-hour night. `catalogue()`, `run()`, `describe()` and `compare()` are the whole surface (#1770, #1777, #1779, #1784, #1801).
