Digital-twin base workflow (#1193): `pinneapple_systems.digital_twin.workflow.digital_twin_workflow`, the `twin.workflow` Flow node and the bundled `digital_twin.json` flow. A state-space model (built-in lumped thermal plant or any `module:function`) and a data source (simulated plant with a known drift and spike, CSV replay, connected stream); an ensemble transform Kalman filter of the states and the log of the slow parameters; NIS against its chi-square band and NEES over independent plants; anomaly gating; a two-sided CUSUM with its stated in-control run length; recalibration through the inverse workflow (refused when its fit fails); a forecast with the 90 % interval, what-if runs and an HTML dashboard.
