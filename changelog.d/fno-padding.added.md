`FourierNeuralOperator`, `FNO2d` and `FNO3d` take `padding`: cells of zeros are appended at the end of each spatial axis after the lifting, and removed before the projection. This is for non-periodic domains, as Li et al. 2021 do for Darcy. The default of 0 leaves existing models unchanged, and the ONNX export accounts for the padded grid. `pinneapple_fno(n, ...)` wraps the library's `FNO2d` as a `GridFieldPredictor`, so it trains and is checked like the PhysicsNeMo models. `examples/fno_coldplate` runs example 07's cold plate with it, without PhysicsNeMo.
