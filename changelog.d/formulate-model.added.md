`pinneapple_problemdesign.formulate`: when no preset models a problem, compose its full physical model from governing laws (battery cell heating with Bernardi's heat generation, ventilated room air with buoyancy, energy and a species, solid conduction, the Arrhenius relation), with the units and source of every law and constant. The draft checks dimensions term by term, closure, conditions on every face and an initial condition, and a manufactured solution through the compiled residual; it is editable with every check re-run, renders LaTeX, and writes the physics-loss (or calibration) code. Reductions (lumped body, steady, 2-D, well mixed) are offered with the number that decides them, or stated when applied. `pinn_problem(..., scales=)` trains a PINN in physical units (inputs mapped to the box, outputs offset and scaled, each equation divided by its largest term); `PINNProblem.output_scales` weighs the condition misfits per output (#1175).
