- `pinneapple_simulation.granular`: a discrete element solver whose contact law acts on the volume two bodies share instead of on a penetration depth, so convex polyhedral particles keep one stiffness per unit of shared volume whatever feature touches. Convex shapes with exact volume, centroid and inertia; the separating axis theorem plus exact overlap volume by half-space clipping; Cundall-Strack friction with a persistent spring, rolling resistance, plane walls and finite obstacles; bonded elements that fail at a strength, with fragments and `P80`; a rotating drum that reports power draw as `torque * omega`, an impulse wear law calibrated against a measured liner profile, and Schiller-Naumann / Di Felice fluid drag wired as a force source. `pinneapple_simulation.granular.lattice` closes the "atoms to material" chain: lattice constant, elastic constants (with the Cauchy relation and Born stability checked), Voigt/Reuss/Hill moduli and a Wulff particle shape. `docs/granular_dem.md` states what is verified, the measured speed and the limits (a single polyhedron in face contact is one contact point; the coupling is one-way; NumPy, not CUDA).
