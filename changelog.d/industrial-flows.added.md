Four closed-loop industrial pipelines in the Flow Studio (#1178): **reactor sizing** (`pinneapple_physics.reactors`: PFR / packed bed with Ergun / CSTR with every steady state and start-up, general Arrhenius kinetics, Kirchhoff heats, Levenspiel, tanks in series, Semenov and Morbidelli-Varma runaway margin, smallest reactor within conversion, hot-spot and runaway limits; flows `reactor_sizing_packed_bed`, `reactor_sizing_cstr`, #1179), **computational lithography** (`pinneapple_physics.lithography`: Abbe and Hopkins SOCS imaging, threshold resist, CD / NILS / EPE / process window, ILT over every focus x dose corner with a mask rule check, a Fourier neural operator checked before use; flow `lithography_opc`, #1180), **wing design** (`pinneapple_design.aero.wing_design`: vortex lattice + friction build-up, least cruise drag within root-bending, stall and span limits, checked against lifting-line theory and Munk's bound; flow `wing_design`, #1181) and the **Ahmed-body design step** (`ahmed_study.design`, node `study.design` in `ahmed_body_end_to_end`: least robust drag inside the sampled box, the drag-crisis band flagged, verified by a close recorded CFD run or stated unverified, #1182).
