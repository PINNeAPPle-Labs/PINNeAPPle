`pinneapple_physics.pinn_solver.compiler.integral_kinds`: two PDE kinds that are not differential equations, `fredholm2` and `volterra2` (second kind, on an interval), solved in Nystrom form with the composite-trapezoid weights on the collocation points themselves and an analytic self-cell integral for the diagonal of a singular kernel (`log_diag`, `helmholtz2_diag`). The kernel and the free term travel in `pde.params` like every other source, `singular=True` refuses to run without a `diag_fn`, and `nystrom_matrix` returns the same discretisation for the classical direct solve. Four presets register the kinds at the `pp` level with closed-form solutions (`integral_fredholm_unit_exp`, `integral_fredholm_separable_exp`, `integral_volterra_unit_exp`, `integral_log_kernel_linear`), catalog methods E72/E73, and `examples/numerical_solvers/14_integral_equations_nystrom.py` measures the orders: second for a bounded kernel, first for a logarithmic one, and the causal mask of Volterra as the difference between a bounded solution and a wrong one (#1866).
