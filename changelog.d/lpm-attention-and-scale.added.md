**Added:** the design-space Large Physics Model (`pinneapple_neural.lpm`, T22) gains the two pieces that separate it from a per-geometry surrogate, both tested and measured. A geometry-aware backbone: `LargePhysicsModel(..., backbone="attention")` adds `PointAttention` blocks, cross-attention from every query to the surface samples of its own design, masked across a batch (Transolver, arXiv:2402.02366; GeoTransolver, arXiv:2507.13822; AB-UPT, arXiv:2505.18781). Batched design-space training: `stack_designs` pads and masks several designs into one `DesignBatch`, `fit(..., batch_designs=N)` trains them in one optimizer step, and the tests pin that a design inside a batch gets the same prediction and the same loss as on its own. `ellipse_design_space(n)` builds a design space with the exact potential-flow Cp, so scale can be measured without a solver: `benchmarks/lpm_scale.py` trains 1000 analytic designs and reports **2.18 % held-out relative L2 (p90 3.35 %)** against the Milne-Thomson §9.61 answer in 775 s on an Apple MPS device, with the OOD flag off on every held-out design (max 1.26× the training spread) and on for all three probes outside the family (square 10.9×, circle r = 3 109.3×, thin ellipse 7.7×). `examples/lpm_design_space` trains it end to end with its `experiment.json`, and the strategy document records what is still missing for a production LPM (`docs/strategy/STRATEGY_PHYSICS_AI.md` §5.3). The same run with the MLP backbone reaches 3.66 % held-out (p90 5.49 %) in 1182 s (`benchmarks/_out/lpm_scale_mlp.json`).
