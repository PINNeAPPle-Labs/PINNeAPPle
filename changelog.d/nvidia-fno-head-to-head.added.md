`examples/nvidia/19_physicsnemo_vs_pinneapple` puts NVIDIA's PhysicsNeMo FNO and the library's own `pinneapple_fno` on the same Darcy problem, with the same training pairs, the same `ComputeBudget` and matched parameter counts (2.37 M each), and judges both with `pinneapple_physics.darcy_checks` inside and outside the training envelope. It is the example that shows what the two libraries are each worth: the operators are comparable, the verdicts are not, and only the checks say when a good-looking error is not the whole story.
