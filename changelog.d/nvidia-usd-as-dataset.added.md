`examples/nvidia/20_usd_is_the_dataset` uses OpenUSD as the dataset, not only as an output. Each transient scan is written as a `.usda` stage with the temperature keyed over the timeline; the PhysicsNeMo training pairs are read back out of those files with `pxr`, the rollout over an unseen scan is written back into its own stage as a second mesh, and the trust decision rides on the prim (`pinneapple:trust:decision`). The example checks the round trip (the file holds exactly the float32 the solver wrote) and that the decision read back from the file is the one that was written.
