- `pinneapple_unity`: Unity integration, in three lanes. **File lane** -- `export_for_unity(scene, folder)` writes a folder a Unity project imports as it is: `Assets/TwinData` (`scene.json`, `geometry.glb`, `fields.bin`, the same three files the web viewer reads) plus `Assets/PinNeAPPleTwin`, a C# runtime that builds one GameObject per part, colours a field per vertex over the scene's time steps, replays rigid motions and per-step visibility, shows the sensors with their series and alarm envelope, and draws the legend; `verify_unity_folder` re-reads the folder (GLB structure, field offsets, package files). **Link lane** -- `from_unity(folder)` reads a scene authored in Unity (`geometry.glb` + `pinneapple_link.json`: parts, units, sensors, `UsdPhysics`, and the *jobs* to run), `apply_link` runs them with their checks, and `unity_to_usd` writes the result back as a twin and as an OpenUSD layer with time-sampled `primvars`, `UsdPhysics` colliders/materials/scene and the provenance in the layer metadata; `emulate_unity_export` writes exactly the two files the Unity editor menu writes, so the lane runs and is tested without Unity. **Live lane** -- `serve_bridge` / `python -m pinneapple_unity serve` is an HTTP bridge (`pinneapple-unity/1`: `/v1/health`, `/v1/run`, `/v1/scene`, optional `X-Pinneapple-Token`, loopback by default, warmed up at start-up) that answers a running Unity client with the field, its checks and its decision (`/v1/scene` also takes `save` and writes the computed twin back as a Unity project folder), plus `RunAndApply` on the C# client and `ApplyField` on the view so a field computed at runtime is shown without writing anything to disk. Three jobs ship: `slab_transient` (Carslaw & Jaeger, checked for residual, walls, maximum principle and the first-mode steady bound), `cantilever_stress` (statics, moment line, yield and Basquin), `fin_temperature` (Incropera 3.4 A, checked against the closed-form heat dissipated); `register_job` adds more. The C# data layer (JSON, GLB read/write, manifest, fields, colormaps) has no `UnityEngine` dependency and is compiled and run against a real exported twin by `tests/test_unity_csharp.py` (skipped without `dotnet`); the MonoBehaviours, the editor menu and the two shaders (Built-in and URP) are compiled by Unity, not by CI. Examples: `examples/unity/` (twin to Unity, Unity to PINNeAPPle to OpenUSD, live bridge).
