- The underground-storage programme has its Flow node (issue #1083's Outputs): `ugs.storage_season` runs one storage season on the reference simulator — the injection and withdrawal bottom-hole pressures, the two durations and how many wells stay shut are the parameters — and reports the average pressure, the gas in place, the peak daily deliverability and the rate, bottom-hole pressure and cumulative of every well on that day, with the mass-balance error of the implicit solve beside them as the check that the panel is a solution. Measured with its defaults (nine-well synthetic model, 12 d injection, 10 d withdrawal, two wells shut): peak 610 419 m3/d on day 15, average pressure 12.00 -> 12.62 MPa, mass-balance error 6.4e-16. It covers `pinneapple_simulation.ugs` in `python -m pinneapple_flow coverage`, which no longer reports the module as having neither a node nor a reason.
