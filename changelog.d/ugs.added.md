- Underground gas storage has a reference simulator and the four numbers that verify it (`pinneapple_simulation.ugs`, issue #1083). It solves single-phase real gas on a structured grid — `Gas` with Papay or Hall-Yarborough z and Lee-Gonzalez-Eakin viscosity, `CartesianGrid` in one to three dimensions or a logarithmic `RadialGrid` with log-normal permeability fields, Peaceman well indices, rate or bottom-hole-pressure control that falls back to the well's limits — with a two-point flux approximation, backward Euler and Newton on an analytic Jacobian and a sparse direct solve. Every run reports what an engineer reads first: average pressure, gas in place, rate and bottom-hole pressure per well, daily deliverability, and a **measured** mass-balance error. On this machine the run is exact to 2.6e-15 of the gas in place, the line-source drawdown agrees with Theis to 3.7e-3 of the drawdown, the closed reservoir declines at `-q/(V phi ct)` to 1.5e-8 with R2 1.0000000000, `p/z` against cumulative production is a line to R2 0.99999935 that recovers the gas in place to 2.1e-3, and the observed orders of accuracy are 1.04 in time and 1.91 in space (nominal 1 and 2). A step that does not converge returns `converged=False` with the time, the iteration count and the reason. `python -m pinneapple_simulation.ugs` runs the whole report in 1.3 s.
- The UGS benchmark and the operations optimisation land with the part that can be measured now (issues #1085 and #1086). The benchmark generates fifteen seasons on a synthetic nine-well model — Latin hypercube over the injection and withdrawal pressures, a spatial gradient and the wells shut in, plus three extrapolation cases (withdrawal below the training range, every well open, both at once) — and scores a **tank (material balance) baseline** against it: 6.3e-2 relative L2 on the field, R2 0.58, up to 6.2 MPa of bottom-hole-pressure error at the wells and 31 % on the daily deliverability, in 0.018 s per scenario against the simulator's 0.159 s. **The neural surrogate is not there**: #1084 (3-D and space-time FNO, factorised spectral weights, Sobolev H1 loss) was not done, so `run_benchmark` reports `surrogate: {"present": false}` with that reason and quotes no speed-up and no break-even it cannot measure — the dataset and the ruler it has to beat are what exist. The optimiser searches the split of the pre-peak demand over the wells on the reference simulator, so every recommendation is verified by construction: on the benchmark model it lifts the peak day from 430 022 to 442 317 m3/d (**+2.9 %**) in 89 simulator runs, meeting the pre-peak demand and the 7 MPa pressure floor with 2.7 MPa to spare, and the equivalent capital is computed rather than quoted — one extra well would reach the same peak, 0.67 of a well by proportion.
