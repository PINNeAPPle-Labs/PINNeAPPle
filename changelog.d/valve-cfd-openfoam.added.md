`pinneapple_systems.process_components.valve_cfd`: the throttling path of a globe valve as an axisymmetric plug-and-seat passage (`ValveGeometry`), meshed with gmsh as a 5° wedge, and an OpenFOAM case written and run by the library. The case uses `rhoPimpleFoam` with local time stepping, `heRhoThermo`, and `PengRobinsonGas` or `perfectGas`. The mixture is one pseudo-pure fluid: Kay's rule for the critical constants, GERG-2008 for the ideal-gas Cp. `valve_cfd_study` runs both equations of state and compares them with the library's throttling at the CFD's own mass flow, then checks convergence, the stagnation-enthalpy balance and the consistency with the case's own gas model, and gives a VeriPhysics decision. On the default case (70 → 20 bar, 25 °C, 8 109 cells): the ideal gas gives 24.30 °C at the outlet (only the kinetic term) and Peng–Robinson −1.81 °C, against −2.57 °C (Peng–Robinson) and −0.20 °C (GERG-2008) from the throttling. The Colab notebook `07_valvula_joule_thomson.ipynb` gains the geometry, the OpenFOAM install on Colab and both runs (#1467, #1464).
