`notebooks/colab/07_valvula_joule_thomson.ipynb`, in Portuguese: Joule-Thomson cooling of natural gas across a globe valve with the open library. It covers the case in words, the outlet temperature by four equations of state, the isenthalpic path and μ_JT, the full study with a CFD outlet temperature checked and graded, sensitivity, and what to change in a CFD that shows no cooling. It runs on Colab from PyPI 0.7.0 plus the three module files from the repository (#1465).
