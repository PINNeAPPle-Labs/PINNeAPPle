`notebooks/colab/07_valvula_joule_thomson.ipynb`, in Portuguese: Joule-Thomson cooling of natural gas across a globe valve with the open library. It covers the case in words, the outlet temperature by four equations of state, the isenthalpic path and μ_JT, the full study with a CFD outlet temperature checked and graded, sensitivity, and what to change in a CFD that shows no cooling. On Colab it installs the library from a shallow sparse clone of the repository (the packages only, about 60 MB), since its modules are newer than the PyPI release (#1465).
