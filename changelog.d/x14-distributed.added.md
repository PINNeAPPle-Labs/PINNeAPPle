`pp.distributed` (`pinneapple_core.distributed`): parallel execution for physics work, not only training. `map`, `sweep` (a failed configuration is recorded in its slot, not raised) and `ensemble` (seeds spawned from one `SeedSequence`, so results do not depend on the worker count) run on a joblib/loky pool when the new `[distributed]` extra is installed, on `concurrent.futures` otherwise, or on threads or in the caller. `partition_mesh` and `map_mesh` split a mesh by recursive bisection with a halo layer, so a nodal operator is exact on the owned nodes of every part; the shares are proportional to the parts each split must produce, because halving balanced only powers of two (five parts of a 252-cell mesh came out as 63, 63, 63, 32, 31 cells). `train_decomposed` trains a PINN by additive Schwarz iteration, with the subdomains of one iteration trained concurrently against their neighbours' previous values. `ensemble` and `map_mesh` send workers module-level functions rather than closures, so they also work without joblib, and the tests cover both paths. On `-u'' = pi^2 sin(pi x)` with two subdomains the relative L2 error is under 2% in about 3 s; four subdomains converge more slowly, Schwarz without a coarse space moving information one subdomain per iteration (#198).
