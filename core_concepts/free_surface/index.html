    Free-surface flow: runners, channels, launders¶

    pp.cfd.FreeSurfaceFlow (pinneapple_simulation.numerical_solvers.free_surface) writes and runs OpenFOAM interFoam cases with two phases separated by a free surface (volume of fluid). It covers:

    • melt runners and launders;
    • spillways and open channels;
    • tanks filled by a jet.
    from pinneapple_simulation.numerical_solvers.free_surface import FreeSurfaceFlow, Phase, SampleLine

flow = FreeSurfaceFlow(
    mesh="cases/runner/mesh",                                   # or regions={name: (V, F)} for snappyHexMesh
    roles={"jet": "inlet", "top": "atmosphere", "exit": "outlet"},       # every other patch is a wall
    inlet_velocity={"jet": (0.0, 9.85, 1.74)},                  # the full vector, m/s
    level=-2.04,                                                 # initial liquid level, height along -gravity
    liquid=Phase("metal", rho=7200, nu=8.472e-6), gas=Phase("air", rho=1.2, nu=1.5e-5), sigma=1.8,
    gravity=(0, 0, -9.8), end_time=30.0, sample_every=0.1, keep_every=1.0,
    lines=[SampleLine("L1", (-1.8, -7.429, -2.2), (-1.8, 15.305, -2.2), 101)])
flow.write("cases/runner/run", procs=4)
flow.run("cases/runner/run", procs=4)                           # setFields, decomposePar, interFoam
prof = flow.profiles("cases/runner/run", t_start=2.0)["L1"]    # times x points, and the time mean

    Boundary conditions¶

    Role Velocity Liquid fraction Pressure k, ω
    inlet the given vector 1 fixed flux from intensity (1 %) and mixing length (0.1 m)
    outlet, atmosphere pressure inlet-outlet inlet-outlet; reverse flow is gas total pressure p_ref (prghTotalPressure) inlet-outlet
    wall no slip zero gradient fixed flux wall functions

    Initial state. The liquid fills everything below level. level is a height along −gravity, so gravity may be tilted, as for a sloped channel meshed in its own frame. hRef is set at the level, so the initial hydrostatic state is in balance. The turbulence model is k-ω SST, or laminar.

    Lines sampled while the solver runs¶

    LiveLineSampler reads each time the solver writes and records the nearest cell's value at every point of every line. There is no spatial interpolation, and points outside the fluid are dropped. It then writes postProcessing/lines/<t>/<line>.csv.

    • No function objects. It does not use OpenFOAM function objects. Their sets and probes types abort in parallel on OpenFOAM v1912.
    • Bounded disk. With keep_every, the times it has sampled are deleted, except one every keep_every seconds and the last. This bounds the disk use of a long transient. In a 30 s run of 180 000 cells, the 300 field writes would otherwise take more than 10 GB.
    • Serial and decomposed cases. It reads both. It also takes a callable instead of a directory, for runners that retry in new directories.

    Example: floor slope of a melt runner¶

    examples/free_surface/01_runner_slope_study.py uses TroughSection (pinneapple_design.geometry.trough): a 2-D section of an 8 m runner with a 0.5 m weir at the downstream end.

    Setup. - A jet enters through an 8 cm slot and reaches the bath at about 5 m/s. - The floor pivots about the weir end, so only the depth under the jet changes: 0.26 m at +2° (floor descending toward the weir), 0.50 m at 0° and 0.74 m at −2°. - The mesh is rectangular in the runner's frame, and the slope enters through tilted gravity. A sloped mesh with vertical gravity diverged in the shallow upstream corner. The tests check that the tilted frame keeps the weir crest, the bath level and the vertical jet speed.

    Results. k is taken 5 cm above the floor and averaged over 4–12 s:

    Slope Metal under the jet Mean k, impact zone 0.5–3 m Relative to 0° Peak k
    +2° 0.26 m 1.40 m²/s² ×2.9 5.0 m²/s²
    0° 0.50 m 0.49 m²/s² ×1 1.6 m²/s²
    −2° 0.74 m 0.20 m²/s² ×0.41 0.40 m²/s²

    A deep pool at impact damps the jet before it reaches the floor, and a shallow one lets it hit the floor almost undamped. The ranking is clear.

    The magnitudes are for screening only: - the section is 2-D, so a slot replaces the round jet; - the turbulence model is RANS with wall functions, on 4 cm cells; - after 12 s the bath is still filling, about 25–35 % more liquid than at the start.

    The study ranks the slopes but does not give a wear rate.

    Tests: tests/test_free_surface.py. They check the region split, the tilted frame, the written case (patch roles, the empty front and back skipped, inlet k from the intensity, a rotated box for setFields, hRef), and a short OpenFOAM run that keeps the floor line in the bath and deletes the sampled times.
