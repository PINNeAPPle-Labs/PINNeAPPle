    Latent-space methods and cybersecurity applications: evaluation and roadmap¶

    Status: October 2026. Two directions were evaluated against what PINNeAPPle already ships, ranked by usefulness and fit, and the top item of each was implemented with an experiment against an independent reference.

    • A. Latent space — latent representations used inside the pipeline (problem → geometry → data → model → trust → decision), not as a stand-alone model zoo.
    • B. Cybersecurity — applications where physics gives something a purely data-driven security tool does not have. Detection and defence only.

    Scoring: usefulness (how often a user of the library would need it, and what it changes in a decision) and fit (how much existing code it reuses, how naturally it sits in the pipeline) are rated 1–5; effort is in person-weeks for a first version with tests, an example and a guide; risk is the chance that the acceptance test fails for reasons other than engineering effort (method immaturity, missing data, compute).

    What exists today¶

    Latent and reduced-order:

    • pinneapple_neural.architectures.rom: POD (randomised, incremental), DynamicModeDecomposition, HAVOK, OperatorInference (quadratic, discrete and continuous), SINDy, KoopmanAutoencoder, NeuralROM, ROMHybrid, DeepUQROM, ParametricPOD (RBF, GP, linear, nearest; amplitude normalisation), and now TrustedLatentSurrogate.
    • pinneapple_neural.architectures.autoencoders (dense, 2-D convolutional, VAE, Koopman and physics-informed Koopman autoencoders, AE-ROM hybrid), continuous.latent_ode, continuous.deep_state_space, neural operators (FNO, DeepONet, MS-DeepONet, GNO), pinns.physics_diffusion (diffusion model with PDE-residual guidance).
    • Trust and uncertainty: pinneapple_analysis.trust (TrustGate: coordinate Mahalanobis OOD, PDE residual, ensemble variance; TrustReport: APPROVED / REVIEW / REJECT), pinneapple_analysis.uncertainty (split conformal with the exact finite-sample quantile, ensembles, MC dropout, calibration), verification.geometry_ood_guardrail (hand-crafted shape features, diagonal Mahalanobis).
    • Assimilation: pinneapple_analysis.state_estimation (EKF, EnKF), data_assimilation.var4d, inverse_problems.ensemble_kalman (EKI).
    • Lab: pinneapple_lab experiment rom_study (POD/DMD/OpInf against the library's solvers).

    Security (pinneapple_security): integrity manifests, Ed25519/HMAC signing and DSSE, in-toto/SLSA attestation and SBOM, hash-chained audit log, AES-GCM at rest, PII detection and pseudonymisation, differential privacy (mechanisms, RDP accountant, DP-SGD), checkpoint scanning and adversarial_sensitivity, anomaly (residual CUSUM, replay score), secrets scanning, data classification, compliance map; and now cps + cps_bench. Related: pinneapple_systems.digital_twin.monitoring.anomaly (Mahalanobis on Kalman innovations).

    What was missing: no latent model carried a calibrated per-query error statement into the trust card, and no security module compared physics against data-driven detection on a reproducible benchmark.

    A. Latent space — ranked¶

    # direction usefulness fit effort (pw) risk verdict
    1 Latent-space trust: conformal error bounds, envelope OOD and decoded-field physics residual for latent surrogates 5 5 1 low implemented
    2 Latent data assimilation (EnKF / 3D-Var in POD or autoencoder coordinates) for digital twins 5 4 2 low next
    3 Latent dynamics surrogates with stability and rollout trust (OpInf / Koopman / latent ODE, energy-preserving variants, horizon-dependent bounds) 4 5 2–3 medium next
    4 Physics-constrained latent spaces (conservation in latent coordinates, latent residual training, structure-preserving projections) 4 3 3 medium later
    5 Geometry latent spaces (shape autoencoders, implicit neural fields, point-cloud encoders) for design optimisation and geometry OOD 4 3 4–6 medium-high later
    6 Latent generative surrogates (latent diffusion / flow matching) with physics checks 3 3 4–6 high research track

    A1. Latent-space trust (implemented)¶

    • State of the art. Normalised split-conformal regression (Lei et al., JASA 2018; Angelopoulos & Bates, arXiv:2107.07511) gives distribution-free, finite-sample error bounds from any spread estimate; conformal prediction has been applied to neural operators and PDE surrogates (e.g. Gopakumar et al., "Uncertainty quantification of surrogate models using conformal prediction", arXiv:2408.09881; Moya et al., conformalised DeepONets, 2024). Feature-space distance as an OOD score is standard (Lee et al., NeurIPS 2018, Mahalanobis in feature space). Residual-based a-posteriori estimators are the classic certification tool of reduced bases (Hesthaven, Rozza & Stamm, 2016).
    • What existed. ParametricPOD with GP standard deviation; TrustGate (coordinate OOD, residual, ensemble) and TrustReport; split conformal in uncertainty.conformal. None of these calibrated a field-level error bound per query for a latent model, nor turned it into a decision.
    • Integration point. model → trust → decision. TrustedLatentSurrogate.trust_report returns a TrustReport; REVIEW/REJECT means "run the solver for this query".
    • Acceptance test (met). On a 2-D advection-diffusion-reaction problem with every answer checked against a full solve: in-envelope coverage of the 90 % bound within [0.85, 1]; error-bound AUC for "error > tolerance" higher than a box check; fewer approved-but-wrong answers than a box check. Measured (three seeds): coverage 0.92; AUC 0.980 (bound), 0.976 (physics residual) vs 0.891 (box); approved-but-wrong 3.3 % vs 8.8 %; median error 0.84 % (nearest-design baseline 17 %); 65× faster than the solver in batch, 18× with all checks. Details: docs/guides/trusted_latent_surrogate.md.

    A2. Latent data assimilation¶

    • State of the art. Assimilation in reduced coordinates: Amendola et al., "Data assimilation in the latent space of a convolutional autoencoder", ICCS 2021; Peyron et al., "Latent space data assimilation by using deep learning", QJRMS 147 (2021); reduced-order EnKF / POD-4D-Var (Cao et al., IJNMF 2007); review by Cheng et al., IEEE/CAA J. Autom. Sinica 10 (2023).
    • What exists. EnsembleKalmanFilter, var4d, POD/AE encoders, latent dynamics (OpInf, Koopman), digital-twin monitoring.
    • Integration point. data → model (twin state update). Encode the ensemble, forecast with the latent dynamics, update with sparse sensors through the decoder (observation operator = sensor sampling of the decoded field).
    • Effort / risk. 2 pw, low: all parts exist; the work is the observation operator through the decoder and inflation/localisation in latent coordinates.
    • Acceptance test. Twin of a 2-D advection-diffusion field observed by 10–20 noisy point sensors: latent EnKF RMSE of the full field ≤ 1.1 × full-space EnKF RMSE at ≥ 20× lower cost per cycle; both against the true simulated field (independent reference); spread–error ratio in [0.7, 1.3].

    A3. Latent dynamics surrogates with stability and rollout trust¶

    • State of the art. Operator inference (Peherstorfer & Willcox, CMAME 2016) and its energy-preserving / stable variants (Sawant, Kramer, Peherstorfer, CMAME 2023; Goyal, Pontes Duff, Benner, 2023); Koopman autoencoders (Lusch, Kutz, Brunton, Nat. Commun. 2018); latent ODEs (Rubanova et al., NeurIPS 2019); LaSDI (Fries, He, Choi, CMAME 2022); latent evolution of PDEs (Wu, Maruyama, Leskovec, NeurIPS 2022); encode-process-decode (Sanchez-Gonzalez et al., ICML 2020).
    • What exists. OpInf, DMD, HAVOK, SINDy, Koopman AE, latent ODE, rom_study lab experiment.
    • Integration point. model + trust: a horizon-dependent conformal bound (calibrated per forecast step) and a stability check (spectral radius / energy growth of the learned latent operator) in the trust card.
    • Effort / risk. 2–3 pw, medium (long-horizon calibration is not exchangeable across horizons; calibrate per horizon).
    • Acceptance test. Cylinder-wake and Burgers datasets from the library's solvers: 200-step rollout error of stable OpInf ≤ unconstrained OpInf and ≤ DMD; per-horizon 90 % bound covers ≥ 85 % of held-out trajectories at every horizon; ≥ 100× speed-up over the solver.

    A4. Physics-constrained latent spaces¶

    • State of the art. Conservation-enforcing nonlinear-manifold ROMs (Lee & Carlberg, "Deep conservation", JCP 2021), structure-preserving (port-Hamiltonian, GENERIC) latent dynamics (Hernández et al., JCP 2021), physics- informed latent training (Kim et al., JCP 2022, "A fast and accurate physics-informed neural network reduced order model"). koopman_pi_ae is a start.
    • Integration point. model training; conserved quantities checked in the trust card.
    • Effort / risk. 3 pw, medium.
    • Acceptance test. On a conservative problem (advection of a scalar, heat in an insulated domain) the decoded total mass drifts < 1e-6 relative over 500 steps (vs ≥ 1e-3 for an unconstrained AE-ROM), with field error no worse than 1.2 × the unconstrained model, against the full solver.

    A5. Geometry latent spaces¶

    • State of the art. DeepSDF (Park et al., CVPR 2019), occupancy networks (Mescheder et al., CVPR 2019), PointNet (Qi et al., CVPR 2017), geometry-informed neural operators (GINO, Li et al., NeurIPS 2023), Transolver (Wu et al., ICML 2024).
    • What exists. geometry_ood_guardrail (hand features), pinneapple_design parametric geometry and optimiser, car2d generator, voxelisation.
    • Integration point. geometry → model (shape code as surrogate input) and geometry → trust (latent density as a geometry OOD score replacing hand features).
    • Effort / risk. 4–6 pw, medium-high (needs a shape dataset and GPU training for 3-D).
    • Acceptance test. On the car2d family: shape-code surrogate drag error ≤ the parametric surrogate's on held-out shapes; latent OOD AUC ≥ 0.9 for shapes from a different family (vs the current guardrail's AUC on the same split).

    A6. Latent generative surrogates¶

    • State of the art. Latent diffusion (Rombach et al., CVPR 2022), flow matching (Lipman et al., ICLR 2023), autoregressive conditional diffusion for turbulence (Kohl, Chen, Thuerey, arXiv:2309.01745), physics-informed diffusion (Shu, Li, Barati Farimani, JCP 2023; Bastek, Sun, Kochmann, ICLR 2025), GenCast (Price et al., Nature 2025).
    • What exists. PhysicsInformedDiffusion with PDE-residual guidance, POD/AE encoders.
    • Integration point. model (ensembles of plausible fields for uncertainty), trust (residual of every sample).
    • Effort / risk. 4–6 pw, high (GPU, evaluation of distributional skill is itself research).
    • Acceptance test. On a turbulent 2-D flow: CRPS of the generated ensemble ≤ that of a deep ensemble of the same cost; every released sample passes the residual check; energy spectrum within 10 % of the solver up to the resolved wavenumber.

    B. Cybersecurity — ranked¶

    # application usefulness fit effort (pw) risk verdict
    1 Physics-residual false-data-injection detection in cyber-physical systems (water, process, HVAC), with a reproducible benchmark 5 5 1.5 low implemented
    2 Power-grid state-estimation FDI detection beyond chi-squared bad-data detection (IEEE test cases, dynamic residuals) 5 4 2–3 medium next
    3 Physics-residual screening of training data against poisoning, tied to dataset provenance 4 5 1.5 low-medium next
    4 Sensor spoofing detection for vehicles and robots (GNSS/IMU/wheel-odometry consistency through vehicle dynamics) 4 3 3 medium later
    5 Adversarial robustness and certification of physics surrogates (Lipschitz bounds, randomised smoothing, physics-consistent attacks) 3 4 2–3 medium later
    6 Federated, differentially private training on engineering data across sites 3 4 3 medium later
    7 Signed, re-verifiable trust reports and simulation results (verify the physics residual on load) 3 5 1 low quick win
    8 Side-channel and process-tampering detection from physical signals (acoustic, power) in manufacturing 2 2 4+ high not now

    B1. Physics-residual FDI detection (implemented)¶

    • State of the art. False data injection against state estimation (Liu, Ning, Reiter, CCS 2009); physics-based detection in process control (Cárdenas et al., ASIACCS 2011); CUSUM on physical residuals and the stealthy-attack bound (Urbina et al., CCS 2016); survey: Giraldo et al., ACM CSUR 51(4) 2018; water-network competition BATADAL (Taormina et al., J. Water Resour. Plann. Manage. 2018).
    • What existed. anomaly.ResidualCUSUM (one residual, no identification), anomaly.replay_score, digital-twin innovation monitoring.
    • What was built. cps.MassBalance and cps.FlowLaw (constants identified from trusted data), cps.PhysicsResidualDetector (per-channel CUSUM, threshold calibrated to a false-alarm budget, blamed channel), cps.CusumAlarm (the same alarm rule for every detector), cps.roc_auc, cps.window_auc, cps.episode_metrics; cps_bench: seeded three-tank water network, six attacks, three data-driven baselines, nominal and shifted operating regimes.
    • Data. Simulated. SWaT and WADI require a signed request; BATADAL is public but its host was not reachable from the build environment. A BATADAL adapter is issue-tracked (see roadmap).
    • Acceptance test (met). At a calibrated budget of one false alarm per day: physics detector episode AUC ≥ 0.95 on every single-sensor attack in both regimes; false alarms per day in the shifted regime ≤ 3 × budget; better than every data-driven baseline in the shifted regime. Measured (three seeds): AUC 1.000 on all five single-sensor attacks in both regimes; shifted-regime false alarms 0.47/day vs 84.4 (AR twin), 32.0 (PCA); in the nominal regime the AR twin matches the AUC (1.0) but localises the attacked sensor far less often (bias: 0.03 vs 0.98). Limits stated: a coordinated, physically consistent replay is invisible to any residual test except at a splice. Details: docs/guides/physics_residual_attack_detection.md.

    B2. Power-grid state-estimation FDI detection¶

    • State of the art. Liu et al. 2009; Kosut et al., IEEE Trans. Smart Grid 2011 (malicious data attacks, GLRT); Manandhar et al., IEEE Systems Journal 2014 (Kalman filter + chi-squared/Euclidean detectors); survey Musleh, Chen, Dong, IEEE Trans. Smart Grid 11(3) 2020.
    • What exists. EKF/EnKF, the cps residual framework, CUSUM; no power-flow solver of its own (pandapower, BSD-3, or MATPOWER cases, BSD-3, would supply IEEE 14/30/118-bus data).
    • Integration point. data (SCADA/PMU) → physics (AC power-flow residual) → PhysicsResidualDetector.
    • Acceptance test. IEEE 14- and 118-bus cases with load profiles: random FDI detected with AUC ≥ 0.95 at 1 false alarm/day; stealthy FDI a = H c shown undetectable by static bad-data detection (chi-squared, as theory predicts) and detected by the dynamic residual with AUC ≥ 0.8 when load dynamics are modelled; compared with an isolation forest and an AR twin on the same measurements.

    B3. Physics-residual screening against data poisoning¶

    • State of the art. Certified defences for data poisoning (Steinhardt, Koh, Liang, NeurIPS 2017); poisoning of scientific ML surrogates is barely studied; physics residuals give a label-free consistency test of each training sample.
    • What exists. Residual functions, TrustGate, integrity manifests and attestation, secrets_scan.
    • Integration point. data → model: before training, every simulated or measured sample is scored by its physics residual; outliers are quarantined and the decision is written to the signed dataset manifest.
    • Acceptance test. Darcy/heat datasets with 1–10 % poisoned samples (scaled fields, swapped labels, smooth perturbations): detection AUC ≥ 0.95 for physics residual vs ≤ 0.8 for a data-only outlier detector; surrogate test error after screening within 10 % of the clean-data model.

    B4. Sensor spoofing detection for vehicles and robots¶

    • State of the art. GPS spoofing of UAVs (Kerns et al., J. Field Robotics 2014); control-invariant attack detection (Choi et al., CCS 2018); SAVIOR (Quinonez et al., USENIX Security 2020).
    • What exists. Vehicle bicycle-model runs, EKF/UKF, the cps framework.
    • Acceptance test. Simulated vehicle with GNSS, IMU and wheel odometry: slow GNSS drift spoofing detected with AUC ≥ 0.95 and median delay ≤ 10 s at 1 false alarm per hour, vs an innovation-chi-squared EKF baseline.

    B5. Adversarial robustness and certification of physics surrogates¶

    • State of the art. Interval bound propagation (Gowal et al., 2018), randomised smoothing (Cohen, Rosenfeld, Kolter, ICML 2019), Lipschitz-constrained networks.
    • What exists. model_security.adversarial_sensitivity (empirical worst case), TrustGate.
    • Acceptance test. For an MLP surrogate of a parametric PDE: certified output radius from IBP or smoothing that upper-bounds the empirical worst case (PGD) on 100 % of 200 test points; a physics-consistent attack (inputs that keep the PDE residual small) found or bounded.

    B6. Federated, differentially private training¶

    • State of the art. FedAvg (McMahan et al., AISTATS 2017), secure aggregation (Bonawitz et al., CCS 2017), DP-SGD with RDP accounting (Abadi et al., CCS 2016; Mironov 2017).
    • What exists. DP-SGD and RDP accountant; no federated loop.
    • Acceptance test. Three sites with different operating regimes training a surrogate: federated DP model at ε ≤ 8 within 1.3 × the error of centralised non-private training and better than every site's local model on the pooled test set.

    B7. Signed, re-verifiable trust reports¶

    • What exists. DSSE signing, attestation, TrustReport.to_dict.
    • Acceptance test. A signed trust report whose recorded physics residual is recomputed on load from the signed model and inputs; any tampering with the report, the model or the inputs fails verification (property test over 100 random byte flips).

    B8. Side-channel / physical-signal tampering detection¶

    Acoustic or power side channels of additive manufacturing (Al Faruque et al., ICCPS 2016) need sensor data and process models PINNeAPPle does not have. Not recommended now.

    Recommended roadmap¶

    1. Done in this change: A1 (TrustedLatentSurrogate) and B1 (cps, cps_bench).
    2. Next quarter: A2 latent data assimilation and B3 poisoning screening (both reuse what exists, low risk, and strengthen the twin and the data stage of the pipeline); B7 as a quick win; the BATADAL adapter for B1.
    3. Then: A3 latent dynamics with rollout trust, B2 power-grid FDI (with pandapower or MATPOWER cases), A4 physics-constrained latent spaces.
    4. Research track, when a GPU budget and datasets exist: A5 geometry latent spaces, A6 latent generative surrogates, B4, B5, B6. B8 is not planned.

    Each remaining item is tracked as a GitHub issue labelled enhancement with its acceptance test.
