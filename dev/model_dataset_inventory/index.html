    Model and dataset inventory for the zoo (2026-10-13)¶

    This is the inventory of every pre-trained model and dataset found across the organisation that could ship in pp.zoo / pp.datasets, with a verdict for each one. Quality is the gate; quantity is not a goal. Anything derived from client work is excluded entirely and is not described here.

    Sources surveyed:

    • this repository (apps/, examples/, lab/, data/, runs/, pinneapple_*);
    • PINNeAPPle-Labs/PINNeAPPle-Benchmark: experiments 02–08 on main, and experiment 09 on branch exp/09-equal-budget.

    The gate¶

    Model. A model ships only if all of the following hold:

    • Its held-out error against an independent reference is documented and reproducible. The reference may be analytic, a classical solver whose own verification is documented, or published data.
    • It passes the physics checks of its trust report: the decision must be APPROVED.
    • It has no known failure on its stated range.
    • Its validity range is recorded.

    The gate is enforced in code. scripts/zoo/build_assets.py computes each card's metrics with the zoo's own evaluate() and runs pp.zoo.validate(). A model that is not APPROVED, or that breaks its acceptance limits, is not written.

    Dataset. A dataset ships only if both hold:

    • It was generated by a verified solver with mesh or convergence evidence, or it comes from a public source with a compatible licence.
    • Its schema, splits and provenance are documented.

    Trust-report physics checks¶

    All four models pass every check (decision APPROVED, score 1.0).

    • airfoil_cst_rans_mlp
    • Lift-curve slope within 15 % of thin-airfoil 2π/rad on the held-out shapes; the worst deviation is 4.9 %.
    • Cl increases with α below stall (α −2…10°).
    • NACA 0012 symmetry: |Cl(0)| and |Cl(2°)+Cl(−2°)| ≤ 0.01 (measured 0.0009).
    • Cd > 0 and finite on 2000 random in-range inputs.
    • heatsink_forced_mlp
    • More airflow, a more conductive material or a wider source never raises the resistance. The share of held-out designs violating each is 0 %, 0.35 % and 0.23 % (limit 1 %).
    • Pressure drop rises with velocity (0 % violating).
    • R > 0 and ΔP > 0.
    • heatsink_natural_mlp
    • A more conductive material, a wider source or more power never raises the resistance. The share of held-out designs violating each is 0 %, 0 % and 0 % (limit 1 %).
    • The power law is checked but not enforced in training, because the engine's own resistance rises with power on 0.5 % of designs (up to +0.7 % for a +5 % step).
    • R > 0.
    • rover_contact_grc1_viper
    • On a 66×59 grid over the whole box there are no violations of:
      • dFz/dz ≥ 0;
      • dFx/ds ≥ 0 for slip ≤ 0.3;
      • the Mohr–Coulomb traction limit;
      • My ≥ R·Fx.

    Where the references come from (independence)¶

    • Airfoil.
    • The training data, the held-out shapes and the NACA runs all come from the same solver and grid. The held-out error is therefore interpolation error.
    • The solver itself is verified:
      • a 3-grid study on NACA 2412 at α 4°: Cd GCI 1.9 % with observed order 2.4, and Cl within 0.02 %;
      • XFOIL: Cl −2.3 %, Cd +4.5 %;
      • lift slope 0.110/° against 0.110/° from thin-airfoil theory;
      • NASA TMR SST, NACA 0012: Cd 0.00812 vs 0.0081.
    • The zoo also stores the XFOIL polar as an independent-solver split.
    • Known bias: drag at high lift reads about 14 % high on the 20k-cell campaign grid. The card states this.
    • Heat sink.
    • The reference is the physics engine the model was trained on.
    • The engine is validated against independent methods:
      • Lee et al. spreading resistance (1–5 %);
      • Stephan developing-flow Nu with ε-NTU (0–5 %);
      • fully developed friction (2–4 %);
      • Elenbaas (2 %);
      • FVM grid convergence (< 2 %).
    • Rover.
    • The reference is the analytic Wong–Reece model, evaluated by quadrature (GL96 vs quad within 3e-8). The held-out set is a disjoint Latin hypercube.
    • Against published single-wheel tests, the Wong–Reece model's drawbar-pull RMSE is 4–14 N (dataset single_wheel_tests). That is larger than the surrogate's own error, and the card says so.

    Candidates not shipped, and why¶

    Models¶

    Candidate Where Verdict and reason
    Airfoil MeshGraphNet (fields + coefficients) Retrained on airfoil_rans_cst_re4e6_fields (scripts/zoo/train_airfoil_gnn.py: 60 epochs, hidden 48, 8 message-passing steps, seed 0, all fixed before training; 332 training runs of 85 shapes) Rejected by the zoo gate (2026-10-17). The coefficients pass every limit set before training: held-out shapes Cl MAE 0.0081, Cd MAPE 1.17 %; NACA reference Cl 0.0081, Cd 1.36 %; XFOIL NACA 2412 Cl 0.010, Cd 3.0 %; lift slope, monotonicity, symmetry and no-slip checks pass. The predicted stagnation pressure reaches Cp 1.21, above Bernoulli's bound of 1.05. The checkpoint is not shipped, and the limits were not relaxed after seeing the test results. Next attempt, with a new configuration fixed before training: more message-passing steps or a wider network, and a pressure-weighted loss. The old apps/aero_optimizer checkpoint stays unshipped: its split cannot be reproduced on the published fields.
    Heat-sink natural-convection surrogate, unconstrained apps/heatsink_sizer/artifacts/surrogate_natural.pt Not shipped; replaced by heatsink_natural_mlp. Its own trust report's verdict is use_with_caution: the conductivity monotonicity has the right sign in only 94.9 % of held-out designs (strict). Under the zoo gate (≤ 1 % violations) that fails a physics check. The cause is that raising k by 5 % lowers the engine's resistance by only 0.13 % (median), below the fit error. The engine itself is monotone in k and in source width on all 400 designs probed.
    Black-hole accretion residual U-Net retrained on blackhole_riaf_density_256 (examples/black_hole_weather/README.md, "Zoo version") Not shipped: fails the mass-conservation check. The reference now has a resolution study, and its data ship as blackhole_riaf_density_256. The best variant (replicate padding, 3-block rollout loss, input noise, mass penalty) beats persistence with 95 % confidence at every lead from 60 to 2000 GM/c³ on the held-out trajectory: skill 0.27 [0.22, 0.31] at 2000 M, MAE 0.056 vs 0.076 for persistence and 0.073 for DMD. Its implied inflow agrees with the truth in sign at every start. But it breaks the training mass envelope in 37 % of frames, and 14–54 % with an extra mass-change penalty. It also loses to persistence at the first 20 GM/c³ frame. Out of distribution (α = 0.3) it beats persistence by 62 % at 2000 M [43, 76 %] and still breaks the envelope (21 %). The data are also not converged in the variability (see the dataset).
    ERA5 weather model, phase 1 examples/weather_forecasting/checkpoints/phase1_step15000_fp16.pt (40 MB) Not shipped. Its README calls it unfinished ("half way", rollout phases not run). z500 RMSE is 397 m at day 3, against 104–125 m for IFS / Pangu / NeuralGCM.
    Burgers PINN data/artifacts/examples/burgers_pinn/burgers_pinn.pt Not shipped. A toy model: rel. L2 is 0.060 against the exact solution with a max error of 0.22 (a 6 % error is not "good"), and it was saved from an mps device.
    Thermal-mass ODE twin (PINN, λ = 10) Benchmark repo, exp. 02 Not shipped. The PINN is no better than persistence (RMSE h6 2.972 °C vs 2.971). No weights are committed.
    Bumper-beam Transolver Benchmark repo, exp. 03 Not shipped. No weights. The classical POD+GP is better on the field (0.0055 vs 0.018). There is no mesh refinement study. The input deck is an Altair example under CC BY-NC 4.0, which is incompatible.
    Terramechanics "robust PINN" (2-D / 8-D) Benchmark repo, exp. 04 Not shipped. No weights. OOD rel. L2 is 0.37–0.68. Monotonicity fails for z up to 0.1 m. The in-range use case is covered by rover_contact_grc1_viper.
    Thermal channel ROM / FNO / DeepONet Benchmark repo, exp. 05 Not shipped. No weights. Pressure rel. L2 is about 0.99 (pressure fails); POD+DMDc diverged. The data now ships as thermal_channel_cfd; a temperature model is a candidate only if it passes the gate on the unseen families.
    Millennium probes (BSD, Hodge, Riemann, …) Benchmark repo, exp. 06 Not shipped. These are mathematical classifiers, not physics surrogates; the self-similar Burgers PINN failed (λ 0.418 vs 0.5). No weights.
    PDR velocity network (TLIO/DIVE) Benchmark repo, exp. 07 Not shipped. No weights. The data is RIDI (5.2 GB, not ours to redistribute), and it is out of the zoo's physics scope.
    Supersonic shock train Benchmark repo, exp. 08 Not shipped. There is no model, and the shock position is not grid-converged (x/H 6.98 / 4.55 / 3.30 on 3 grids).
    Equal-budget FNO / DeepONet / PINN (Burgers, Darcy, NS 2-D, airfoil, inverse heat) Benchmark repo, exp. 09 Not shipped yet. Only result tables are committed; checkpoints are gitignored. The FNO results are strong (Burgers 0.0033, NS 0.0084 rel. L2), and the generators are verified (Cole–Hopf 3e-13, Taylor–Green 1.7e-15), so these are the best v2 candidates. They will be retrained once pp.benchmark (branch feat/benchmark-api) is merged, because their data generators live there. Darcy is excluded: its OOD-shift check failed. All PINN rows failed (Darcy 0.87, airfoil Cd 99 %).
    runs/bench_PipeVanillaPINN.best.pt this repo Not shipped. A 1-epoch smoke-test artefact (val R² −0.23).
    pinneapple_worldmodel.ModelZoo, pinneapple_hub.from_pretrained this repo These are containers, not models: nothing was registered. The zoo reuses ModelCard, the trust report and ModelStore instead.

    Lab: rom_study / beam_parametric (POD + RBF / GPR cantilever ROM) Candidate, not shipped. Its error on unseen designs is measured against FEM on the same mesh, and the FEM has no mesh study. It ships once a mesh study bounds the FEM error and a zoo adapter for ParametricPOD exists.
    Fin / plate inverse-convection fields Not shipped. These are outputs of a single case, not a dataset.
    pinneapple_data.datasets registry entries Not shipped as zoo items. They are generated on the fly. Several real_world entries silently fall back to synthetic placeholders. Zoo datasets are registered in that registry as zoo/<name>.
    ERA5 regional subset (rs_floods_2024_40.npy) Not shipped. It has no documented splits, and its companion model is unfinished.
    Shock-train fields (exp. 08) Not shipped: not grid-converged.
    Bumper-beam DoE (exp. 03) Excluded. It is 11 GB, not in git, and its input deck is CC BY-NC.
    Free-surface trough package Not shipped. It is 4 interFoam runs, not a dataset, and the 5 mm and 10 mm meshes settle into different flow states (not mesh-converged).

    Everything excluded for client provenance has been left out of this document on purpose.

    How to add a model or dataset¶

    1. Make it pass the gate above, with the evidence in the repository.
    2. Add it to pinneapple_hub/zoo/_catalog.py (card or dataset description, acceptance limits, guard metrics) and, for a model, an adapter in _models.py (predict, metrics, physics checks, fine-tuning hooks).
    3. Run python scripts/zoo/build_assets.py --rover DIR. This regenerates the assets, cards (with metrics and trust report) and the manifest; the build is deterministic. To add one item without touching the published ones, use --only NAME (with the training directory of a model trained by its own script, e.g. --heatsink-natural DIR) or --hub-only STAGING_DIR (Hub-only datasets).
    4. Run pytest tests/test_model_zoo.py.
    5. After the merge, the publish workflow uploads to the Hub.
