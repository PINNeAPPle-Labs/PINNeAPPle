## Summary

A 3-D density-based topology optimisation of a load-carrying bracket: minimise mass under a von Mises stress bound, then re-solve the extracted optimum in CalculiX at twice the element density. Every Pareto point is checked by a code other than the one that produced it.

## Question

Does a 3-D SIMP optimum with a stress constraint still meet its stress limit and mass budget when re-meshed and re-solved by CalculiX at twice the element density?

## Why run it

An optimiser checked only by the code that produced it is a closed loop. The value is the cross-code gap in predicted stress, a 3-D workflow ending in a re-verified STL, and the density evolution plus von Mises field for the video.

## Physical model

Linear isotropic elasticity, `div(sigma) + b = 0`. Minimise `m(rho) = sum rho_e V_e` subject to `p-norm(sigma_vm(rho)) / sigma_lim - 1 <= 0`, with SIMP `E(rho) = E_min + rho^3 (E_0 - E_min)`, a density filter of radius `r_min` and Heaviside projection.

Regime groups: allowable strain `sigma_lim / E <= 1e-2`; filter ratio `r_min / h >= 1.5`. Plasticity, fatigue, residual stress and buckling are absent; a member thinner than three elements is reported unresolved.

## Geometry and inputs

A rectangular block with a fixed support patch and one or two loaded patches, from `geometry.gen.sdf_shapes` primitives meshed with `solid_fem.box_mesh`. Load cases: tip load, torsion, combined. Density is contoured at `rho = 0.5`, smoothed, exported as STL. Material `E = 210 GPa`, `nu = 0.3`, `sigma_lim = 120 MPa`; volume fractions 0.15 to 0.45.

## Solver and engine

`pinneapple_design.topology.optimize` with `optimizer="mma"` and the stress constraint as a callable `g(x_phys)` with its gradient, through the driver's existing extra-constraint interface. Re-analysis: `pinneapple_simulation.external_solvers.calculix`. If CalculiX is absent, `numerical_solvers.elasticity3d_fdm`, a weak check because it shares the optimiser's grid.

## What is missing (the work)

1. A 3-D SIMP problem class beside `Compliance2D`: Hex8, three DOF per node, per-element stress recovery, assembled sparse solve.
2. A 3-D density filter and symmetry map compatible with `DesignChain`.
3. A stress aggregation module: p-norm or Kreisselmeier-Steinhauser over element von Mises, with its adjoint.
4. A greyness gate: intermediate-density measure and refusal to export above threshold.
5. A density-to-STL extractor with smoothing and watertightness check, and the CalculiX round trip from the extracted surface.
6. A Pareto driver over (volume fraction, stress aggregate) recording every point.

## Experiment protocol

1. Verify the element against `cantilever_hex_mesh` deflection and `geo_plate_with_hole` (Kt = 3), then three-grid GCI on the unoptimised forward problem.
2. Optimise on 160x80x80 (~1.0 M hex, ~3.0 M DOF): three volume fractions, three load cases, nine runs, `beta_max = 32`, change tolerance 1e-3.
3. Extract, remesh in CalculiX at `h` and `h/2`, re-solve, compare.
4. Log objective and volume history, greyness, aggregation error, wall-clock.

Three perturbed initial densities per case test initialisation dependence. Budget: 200-600 CPU-hours optimisation, 20-60 CPU-hours CalculiX.

## Baselines, references and verification

Baselines: uniform-density block at equal mass; hand-designed ribbed bracket; the optimum without the stress constraint. Verification: closed-form cantilever deflection, Kt = 3, three-grid GCI on `sigma_99`. Peak nodal stress at a re-entrant corner is singular and is logged as a diagnostic only.

## Metrics

Mass fraction `m/m_0` (-). `sigma_99`, the 99th percentile element von Mises (Pa), with `sigma_max` (Pa) as diagnostic. Greyness `G = mean(4 rho (1-rho))` (-). Aggregation error `|p-norm - max|/max` (-). GCI on `sigma_99` (%). Cross-code gap `|sigma_99^opt - sigma_99^ccx| / sigma_99^ccx` (%). Re-solved mass `m_ccx` (kg).

## Deliverables

Density fields as `.npz` (~200 MB); extracted STLs; CalculiX `.inp`/`.frd` pairs; Pareto CSV with GCI per point; figures and a 20-30 s evolution video; the Lab manifest with hashes.

## Acceptance criteria

- The `h/2` re-solve changes `sigma_99` by less than 5 % relative to `h`.
- No element in the re-solved model exceeds `0.95 sigma_lim`.
- Re-solved mass within 2 % of the optimiser's predicted mass.
- Greyness `G < 0.10` on the exported geometry.
- Aggregation error below 3 %, logged for every run.

## Realizability and evidence class

Fills `mathematical_consistency`, `numerical_stability`, `physical_plausibility`. `experimental_validation` is NOT ASSESSED: no coupon is tested. `technological_feasibility` at most CONDITIONAL: print distorsion and feature size against a process are not modelled. `uncertainty_sensitivity` partially assessed via a material-property perturbation.

## Risks and failure modes

1. Re-entrant-corner singularity makes peak stress mesh-dependent; mitigation: report percentile and fixed-radius averaged stress.
2. Grey material neither solid nor void; mitigation: the greyness gate, projected field the only exported artefact.
3. Voxel-to-surface extraction changes the object; mitigation: report the extracted volume change.
4. CalculiX absent; mitigation: the labelled fallback.
5. Local minima; mitigation: report the spread across initialisations.

## Out of scope

- Fatigue, plasticity and buckling of the optimum.
- Multi-material or anisotropic material.
- Manufacturability constraints.
- Dynamic and modal response.
