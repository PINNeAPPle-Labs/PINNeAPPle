## Summary

A mixer geometry optimised to homogenise two inlet streams: minimise the outlet coefficient of variation of a passive scalar at fixed pressure drop. The work is a two-inlet case, a design loop over the mixing elements, and a comparison with a Kenics static mixer of equal length.

## Question

At Peclet number 10^4 and the pressure budget of a six-element Kenics mixer, does the optimised geometry push outlet scalar coefficient of variation below 5 %?

## Why run it

Mixing is the cheapest test of a geometry optimiser whose objective is an integral over an exit plane rather than a force. It yields the pressure cost of each unit of homogeneity, which is what a plant buys. The scalar field is also directly renderable.

## Physical model

Steady incompressible flow with a passive scalar, advected by the mean flow and diffused by molecular plus turbulent diffusivity.

Governing groups: `Re = U D_h / nu`, `Pe = U D_h / D`, `Sc = nu / D`. Pe sets the regime. At 10^4 the scalar layer is thinner than any affordable mesh, so the turbulent path uses the RANS eddy diffusivity: the result is a model output, not a resolved field. Absent: reactions, stream density differences, free surfaces, non-Newtonian rheology.

## Geometry and inputs

A duct of 20 hydraulic diameters, two inlet patches with `c = 1` and `c = 0` at equal flow, one outlet. Design variables: element count, twist sign, spacing, baffle count. Fluid: water at 20 C, `nu = 1e-6 m^2/s`, `D = 1e-9 m^2/s`, Pe from 10^3 to 10^5 over the sweep.

## Solver and engine

`numerical_solvers.internal_flow.InternalFlow` writes the OpenFOAM case (blockMesh O-grid, snappyHexMesh on the element STL) and runs `simpleFoam` then `scalarTransportFoam` on converged fluxes, needing an OpenFOAM install through `external_solvers.openfoam`. Fallback: `fvm`, `stable_fluids` or `lbm`, none of which advects a scalar in 3-D, so it is 2-D and labelled.

## What is missing (the work)

1. A two-inlet configuration: separate patches with `c = 1` and `c = 0` at a prescribed split, replacing the half-inlet construction.
2. A parametric mixer description able to regenerate the STL and the case per trial.
3. An optimisation driver that records every case and its metric.
4. A mixing-cost metric, pressure drop times flow per unit CoV reduction; nothing computes it today.
5. A scalar transport path in the in-process 3-D solvers.
6. A scalar mass-balance check on the outlet plane.

## Experiment protocol

1. Verify the empty duct against the `colebrook` friction factor, Re 100 to 10^4.
2. Three-mesh GCI on outlet CoV at Pe = 10^4.
3. Baselines: empty chamber, Kenics at 3, 6, 12 elements, one baffle.
4. Sweep ~60 mixer geometries at Pe = 10^4.
5. Re-solve the best three at Pe = 10^3 and 10^5, then on a 2x mesh.

Budget 300-900 CPU-hours. Each case logs geometry parameters, cell count, iterations, flow balance, scalar mass balance, axial CoV profile and pressure drop.

## Baselines, references and verification

Baselines: empty chamber; Kenics at three element counts of equal length from `kenics_elements`; one baffle. Verification: Poiseuille friction factor, scalar mass conservation, grid convergence of CoV, and the laminar stratified limit with no mixing. Published mixer data serve as an order-of-magnitude band, not a target.

## Metrics

`CoV = std(c)/mean(c)` at the outlet (-). Pressure drop `dp` (Pa). Mixing energy cost `dp * Q` (W). Normalised cost, the ratio to Kenics at equal CoV (-). Mass balance error (%). GCI on CoV (%). Development length to CoV < 0.05 (diameters).

## Deliverables

Best mixer STL; scalar and velocity fields at three axial stations; CoV-versus-length curves; pressure-drop table; a 15-30 s video of the scalar field; case folders and manifest.

## Acceptance criteria

- Outlet CoV below 0.05 at Pe = 10^4 with `dp` no greater than the six-element Kenics.
- Scalar mass balance closes to 1e-6 relative.
- The 2x mesh changes outlet CoV by less than 5 %.
- Every reported point carries its case folder, cell count and residuals.

## Realizability and evidence class

Fills `mathematical_consistency`, `numerical_stability`, `physical_plausibility`. `experimental_validation` is NOT ASSESSED: no dye test is run. `technological_feasibility` is CONDITIONAL: cleanability, fouling and printability are not modelled. `uncertainty_sensitivity` is partially assessed through the Pe sweep.

## Risks and failure modes

1. Numerical diffusion mimics mixing; mitigation: TVD limiters, first-order contribution reported, refinement on every winner.
2. The RANS eddy diffusivity is a closure choice; mitigation: report the turbulent Schmidt number and its sensitivity.
3. The optimiser exploits the outlet boundary condition; mitigation: extend the outlet duct and check CoV sensitivity.
4. An old STL is silently re-run; mitigation: hash the STL into the case record.

## Out of scope

- Reactive mixing and yield.
- Non-Newtonian or multiphase streams.
- Manufacturability and cleanability.
- Transient oscillating mixers.
