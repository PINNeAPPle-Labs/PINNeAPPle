## Summary

A parametrised compressor cascade optimised on isentropic efficiency, then checked off-design. Design variables are stagger, camber, thickness and lean; the evaluation is RANS on a periodic single passage, cross-checked against a meanline design at equal solidity.

## Question

Does a blade row optimised on a cascade RANS evaluation exceed the efficiency of the meanline design of the same duty and solidity by more than the numerical uncertainty of the comparison?

## Why run it

Meanline design is fast, defensible and blind to three-dimensional loss. A cascade optimiser is the cheapest way to ask whether that freedom is worth anything at this duty. It produces an efficiency curve with a stated convergence band, and blade-to-blade fields that render well.

## Physical model

Steady compressible RANS in the relative frame with a two-equation closure. Rothalpy is constant along a streamline in the rotor frame in the inviscid limit, which fixes the efficiency definition.

Governing groups: flow coefficient `phi = V_x/U`, loading `psi = dh_0/U^2`, `Re = U c/nu`, inlet Mach, solidity `c/s`. Absent: transition modelling, resolved tip clearance, cooling, mistuning. RANS at this Reynolds number over-predicts attached-flow efficiency and under-predicts corner separation; the report must say it cannot tell which side of the truth the answer falls on.

## Geometry and inputs

A linear cascade, one passage with pitch-periodic boundaries. Each section is a NACA four-digit camber and thickness distribution plus stagger and lean, from `geometry.gen.airfoil.naca_parametric`. Duty: pressure ratio 1.20, inlet Mach 0.6, `phi = 0.5`, `Re = 4e5` on chord, matching `AIRFOIL_REYNOLDS`.

## Solver and engine

`external_solvers.openfoam` runs the case through `OpenFOAMCaseTemplate`, but no rotating-zone or pitch-periodic template exists today, so the OpenFOAM leg needs an MRF case on that template. The meanline baseline is `turbodesigner.run_turbodesigner`; surrogate and optimiser are `design.aero.surrogate` with `design.aero.optimize.Engine`.

## What is missing (the work)

1. A rotating-zone (MRF) or pitch-periodic single-passage OpenFOAM template with the correct relative-frame inlet conditions.
2. A blade parametrisation with stagger, camber, thickness and lean as named variables, writing a watertight STL per trial.
3. Post-processing: mass-averaged total pressure and temperature across inlet and outlet, isentropic efficiency, blade torque, and the numerical uncertainty of each.
4. An extrapolation guard: a distance measure to the training set, with the optimiser forbidden from proposing points beyond a stated margin.
5. An optimisation driver that records every case and refuses unhashed geometry.

## Experiment protocol

1. Verify the RANS path on a NACA 4412 cascade against `panel_solve` in the attached regime.
2. Three meshes and GCI on efficiency at the design point.
3. DOE of 60 cases from `airfoil_space`, Latin hypercube over four design variables.
4. Fit the surrogate, optimise, re-solve the top five candidates in RANS.
5. Off-design: optimum and meanline swept over `phi` from -20 % to +20 %, seven points each.

Budget 400-1200 CPU-hours. Each case logs geometry variables, cell count, residuals, mass-flow balance, efficiency, torque and surrogate distance.

## Baselines, references and verification

Baselines: the Turbodesigner meanline blade at equal solidity; the unoptimised NACA 4412 cascade; the best point of a random search with the same RANS budget. Verification: panel method in the attached regime, mass and energy balance across the cascade, GCI on efficiency. The meanline is a sanity band, not a target.

## Metrics

Isentropic efficiency `eta = (h01 - h02)/(h01 - h02s)` (%). Pressure ratio (-). Flow coefficient `phi` (-). Mass flow (kg/s). Blade torque per unit span (N m/m). GCI on `eta` (%). Off-design margin, the change in `phi` before efficiency drops two points (%).

## Deliverables

Blade STL and section coordinates; efficiency-versus-`phi` curves for the optimum and the meanline; design-point Mach and entropy fields; the DOE table; the convergence report.

## Acceptance criteria

- RANS efficiency at the design point is within two points of the meanline for the same geometry.
- The optimised blade exceeds the meanline by more than twice the GCI, or the report says it does not.
- The 2x mesh changes design-point efficiency by less than one point.
- Mass and energy balances close to 1e-3 relative.

## Realizability and evidence class

Fills `mathematical_consistency`, `numerical_stability`, `physical_plausibility`. `experimental_validation` is NOT ASSESSED: no cascade tunnel data are used. `technological_feasibility` is CONDITIONAL: tip clearance, cooling and tolerance are not modelled. `uncertainty_sensitivity` is assessed through the closure and grid bands only.

## Risks and failure modes

1. Closure error exceeds the optimiser's gain; mitigation: report the gain against the GCI band and refuse to claim a win inside it.
2. The surrogate extrapolates to an unphysical section; mitigation: the hull-distance guard and re-solve-before-claim.
3. The periodic interface leaks mass; mitigation: a mass-balance gate on every case.
4. Inconsistent averaging inflates efficiency; mitigation: mass-weighted averaging on both sides.

## Out of scope

- Multistage matching and rotor-stator interaction.
- Cooling, tip clearance and blade root stress.
- Aeroelasticity and flutter.
