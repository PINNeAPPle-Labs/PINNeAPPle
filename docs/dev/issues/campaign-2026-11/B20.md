## Summary

MECHAMORPH is a chain of rigid links that walks to a gap edge, locks its joints, then carries load as a span. Locomotion is rigid-body dynamics with foot–soil contact; the locked state is a static analysis of the same members. Such a chain spans less than a truss of equal mass, by a measured factor.

## Question

For a chain of N links that must walk to a gap of span L, what is the largest L keeping utilization U <= 1 under a specified midspan load?

## Why run it

It makes failure attributable: if the span breaks, the cause is joint stiffness or member section, not the gait. It turns the promise into a mass penalty and a span limit.

## Physical model

Walking: M(q) qdd + C qd + g = tau + J_c^T lambda, with lambda from compliant contact; feet follow Bekker–Wong, p = (k_c/b + k_phi) z^n, tau_s = (c + p tan phi)(1 - exp(-j/K)). Locked: K u = f with locked joints carrying full moment, giving sigma_vm and deflection delta.

Groups: Fr = v/sqrt(g l_leg); L/r_g; U = sigma_vm/sigma_y; kappa = m_lock/m_member; delta/L.

Valid for rigid links, small strain, linear elasticity. It omits plasticity, fatigue, backlash and buckling, so v1 is optimistic for thin members. Contact is penalty-enforced: a completed step does not prove the foot did not sink.

## Geometry and inputs

Parametric serial chain, N in {6, 8, 10}; tube members, outer diameter and wall thickness as variables; N-1 limited revolute joints. Gap terrain from a procedural height field (`scenarios.flat_site` convention) with a trench cut. Load: chain weight plus one midspan point load.

## Solver and engine

`pinneapple_simulation.multibody.tree` (`MultibodyTree`, `RevoluteJoint`) drives both phases; `multibody.arm.fixed_base_forward`, `energy_balance` roll out the gait; foot reaction from `numerical_solvers.bekker_wong.BekkerWongSolver` with `terramechanics.SoilParams`. The span check runs in `numerical_solvers.solid_fem` (`box_mesh`, `SolidFEM`, `von_mises`).

## What already exists in PINNeAPPle (verified)

`multibody.tree` with `Body`, `RevoluteJoint`, `Kinematics`; `multibody.arm.fixed_base_forward`, `energy_balance`; `bekker_wong.BekkerWongSolver`; `design.bricks.Assembly`, `analyse_assembly`, `closed_form.beam_midspan_deflection`; `solid_fem`, `plane_stress_fem.solve_plane_stress`; `verification.convergence.mesh_independence_study`; `pinneapple_lab.experiment` with `log_metric`, `check`, `add_file`.

## What is missing (the work)

1. A lockable joint in `multibody.tree`: rotational DOF switching to zero at a scheduled state, reporting the reaction moment. `RevoluteJoint.limits` is a penalty spring, not a rigid lock.
2. One objective over both phases, so gait speed cannot be bought with a span that fails.
3. A statically stable gait planner, including the bank-edge pose.
4. A structural check at every intermediate pose of the lock sequence.
5. A mass and power budget for joints, locks and actuators, so kappa is computed.
6. A Euler buckling check.

## Experiment protocol

Matrix: N x L in {1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0} m x two soils = 30 cases, five seeds, 150 rollouts; survivors get three FEM levels at ratio 2. Policy: dt = 1e-4 s, energy residual below 2 %, GCI_fine < 5 %. Budget 300–600 CPU-hours. Logged: 1 kHz state, per-foot force and slip, joint torque, lock time, deflection, stress field, commit, input hash.

## Baselines, references and verification

All joints locked must reproduce a ballistic trajectory to 1e-6 relative. All-locked deflection is checked against `beam_midspan_deflection` within 2 %. Competitors: a truss of equal mass carried to the gap; the chain with no lockable joints; a deployable hinged at third points.

## Metrics

Traverse time T [s]. Specific transport energy [J/(kg m)]. Deflection delta [mm] and delta/L. Utilization U. Mass penalty kappa. Failure span L_fail [m]. GCI on delta and stress.

## Deliverables

Thirty case records (JSON, plus npz with 100 Hz trajectory and stress fields), under 2 GB. Figures: U versus span; deflection versus mass for chain and truss. Video with case id and commit per frame. Manifest and re-run parameters from `pinneapple_lab.experiment`.

## Acceptance criteria

- All-locked deflection matches `beam_midspan_deflection` within 2 %.
- GCI_fine below 5 % on peak deflection and stress; walking energy residual below 2 %.
- For at least three spans the report states a span with U <= 1 and its load, or the span and failure mode; total failure is a result.
- kappa is reported for every configuration.

## Realizability and evidence class

PLAUSIBILITY_CLASS today: `extrapolated`. Deployable bridges are built engineering; a walking chain that becomes a span is not, but nothing in the mechanism is excluded at these spans.

To move up one class, ranked: a lockable joint with measured stiffness and backlash; a section buildable at the required slenderness; an actuator mass fraction below the needed kappa.

Filled axes: `mathematical_consistency`, `numerical_stability`, `physical_plausibility`. Not assessed: `experimental_validation` (no specimen), `technological_feasibility` (hardware, wear, power density). `uncertainty_sensitivity` is partly fillable with `margin_study`.

## Risks and failure modes

- Contact stiffness chosen for stability hides penetration — log it and reject runs above a stated fraction of foot size.
- The lock impulse perturbs peak stress — ramp the lock and report ramp sensitivity.
- The optimiser degenerates the machine into a truss — shared member set, published kappa.
- Mesh too coarse at the joint, or Bekker–Wong parameters extrapolated beyond range — submodel and state the parameter source.

## Out of scope

- Hardware, wear, backlash, fatigue life.
- Plastic collapse and post-buckling.
- Stowing, deployment, transport logistics.
