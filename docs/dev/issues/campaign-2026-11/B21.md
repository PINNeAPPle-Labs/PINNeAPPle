## Summary

VORTEX GATE is an annular nozzle array that imposes swirl on the air a body passes through; the experiment measures the lateral force that swirl actually delivers. The physics is transient incompressible flow with two-way body coupling. The deliverable is a ratio of lateral force to body weight, and the verdict follows from that ratio.

## Question

For a body of mass m at speed U crossing a swirl region of diameter D, can the flow deliver a lateral force exceeding the body's weight?

## Why run it

It replaces an argument about whether a flow deflects something with a momentum accounting that either closes or does not. It sets the nozzle power below which the mechanism is irrelevant.

## Physical model

Incompressible Navier–Stokes, du/dt + u.grad u = -grad p/rho + nu lap u, div u = 0, with the body as a moving immersed boundary; m dV/dt = F + m g, I domega/dt = M. Swirl number S = integral(rho u_theta u_x r dA)/(R integral(rho u_x^2 dA)); Re = U D/nu; C_L = 2 F_lat/(rho U^2 A_ref); Pi = F_lat/(m g).

Bounds are computed, not assumed: F_lat <= mdot v_theta and F_lat <= rho U Gamma; a force above either is an accounting error.

Valid for continuum, single-phase, incompressible flow at Re above a few thousand; it leaves out free-stream turbulence, compressibility, ground effect and acoustic radiation.

## Geometry and inputs

Annular manifold of N nozzles (N in {8, 16, 32}) on a ring of diameter D in {0.3, 0.6, 1.0} m, injection angle alpha in {15, 30, 60} deg, plenum pressure as the actuation variable. Body: a sphere and a cube of size D/6, 0.2–5 kg, released upstream offset from the axis (`gen.primitives`).

## Solver and engine

`numerical_solvers.immersed_boundary_fdm.solve_ibm_navier_stokes` for the flow, in a coupling loop with `pinneapple_simulation.flight.rigid_body` (`state_vector`, `rigid_body_derivative`, `rk4_step`). Cross-checks: `numerical_solvers.cylinder_wake` at matched Re, and OpenFOAM through `numerical_solvers.external_flow.run_case` for the highest-swirl case.

## What already exists in PINNeAPPle (verified)

`solve_ibm_navier_stokes`, `solve_ibm_external_flow`, `field_divergence`, `interpolate_to_points` in `immersed_boundary_fdm`; `cylinder_wake.simulate`, `probe_strouhal`, `verify`; `external_flow.run_case`, `CaseFields`; `lbm.LBMSolver`; `flight.rigid_body`; `verification.convergence.mesh_independence_study`; `pinneapple_lab.experiment` with `log_metric`, `check`, `add_file`.

## What is missing (the work)

1. Two-way coupling. `solve_ibm_external_flow` takes a static `wall_points` cloud; nothing moves it. The loop — integrate body, re-impose boundary, recompute force — goes in a new module.
2. Control-surface accounting: a flux integral of momentum plus pressure, reconciled against body traction.
3. A swirl inlet boundary condition with a mass-flow-preserving profile.
4. A circulation estimator for the Kutta–Joukowski bound.
5. Nozzle power accounting, so cost per newton is reported.

## Experiment protocol

Matrix: N x D x alpha x U in {5, 10, 20} m/s x two bodies = 162 cases plus 6 baselines, two seeds. Force averaged over at least 10 flow-through times D/U, drift under 2 %; three grid levels at ratio sqrt(2) for GCI on C_L. Budget 400–800 CPU-hours. Logged: trajectory at 1 kHz, force history, closure residual, GCI, commit.

## Baselines, references and verification

Both bounds are evaluated from the same fields and plotted with the measured force. Unforced sphere drag must match the standard drag correlation within 5 %; if not, the solver is the result. Flux closure against body traction within 3 %. Competitors: no jets; a uniform annular jet; a vane.

## Metrics

C_L [-]. F_lat [N]. Force ratio Pi [-]. Jet power P_jet [W]. Cost F_lat/P_jet [N/kW]. Trajectory offset Delta y/D [-]. GCI on C_L [%].

## Deliverables

162 records and 6 baselines (JSON, plus npz with force history and a mid-plane slice), under 5 GB. Figures: Pi versus S with both bounds; Delta y/D versus U. Video of the strongest case. Manifest and verdict as one sentence.

## Acceptance criteria

- Flux integral closes against body traction within 3 %.
- F_lat below both bounds, or the case is flagged.
- GCI_fine on C_L below 5 % for the three cases that decide the verdict.
- Pi is stated per body and speed, against a threshold registered before the forced results are read.
- NOT FEASIBLE for a stated mass and speed is a successful outcome, delivered as text.

## Realizability and evidence class

PLAUSIBILITY_CLASS today: `hypothetical`. Swirl generators and lateral jet control are established; a ring that steers a fast free body is not.

Filled: `mathematical_consistency`, `numerical_stability`, `physical_plausibility`. NOT ASSESSED: `experimental_validation`, `technological_feasibility`. `uncertainty_sensitivity` is partly fillable.

To move up one class, ranked: a nozzle power density that makes P_jet reasonable against F_lat; a swirl that survives the body's wake; a body slow enough for Pi of order one.

## Risks and failure modes

- The coupling leaks momentum and the force looks larger than it is — the closure check gates all results.
- Time averaging hides a fluctuation larger than the mean — report the standard deviation.
- Confinement adds blockage that inflates the force — state the blockage ratio.
- The device works only at low body speed — stratify by speed, never average across it.

## Out of scope

- Compressible, rarefied or multiphase flow, and multi-body interaction.
- Any claim that such a device could stop an aircraft.
