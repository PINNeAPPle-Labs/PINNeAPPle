## Summary

ORBITAL INFLATION is a stowed structure that deploys into a ring, stiffens, then spins up to provide apparent gravity. Deployment order and spin-up profile are optimised so that no member is overstressed and the ring stays controllable at every instant. The gravity is centrifugal acceleration in a rotating frame; its gradient and Coriolis consequences are reported.

## Question

For a ring of radius R and areal density sigma_a, what spin-up profile keeps peak stress below yield while the gravity gradient across a body stays inside a stated comfort bound?

## Why run it

It converts a habitat sketch into two coupled schedules: fold order and ramp rate, with numbers on both.

## Physical model

Rigid rotation: g_app = omega^2 R; hoop stress in a free thin ring sigma = rho omega^2 R^2 = sigma_a omega^2 R; spin energy E = 0.5 I omega^2, I = m R^2; gradient over a body spanning +/-h at the rim, Delta g/g ≈ 2h/R; Coriolis acceleration 2 omega x v, deflecting a thrown object by order omega v t^2.

Groups: Omega = omega sqrt(R/g0); U = sigma/sigma_y; slenderness; deployment strain.

Valid for rigid rotation, small strain, thin-ring membrane stress, no pretension. It leaves out wrinkling, buckling, flutter, creep and every physiological claim; 2 rpm is a design input, not a measured threshold.

## Geometry and inputs

Torus generated in `pinneapple_twin3d.scene`; R in {50, 100, 150, 250} m; tube or channel section; areal density in {2, 5, 10} kg/m^2; a stated stowed envelope. Deployment is an ordered list of fold events; spin-up is omega(t) piecewise linear with a stated ramp rate and coast.

## Solver and engine

Structure: `numerical_solvers.solid_fem` (`box_mesh`, `SolidFEM`, `von_mises`) on a hexahedral ring sector, with `elasticity3d_fdm.solve_static_elasticity_3d` as an independent check. Orbital context: `nbody_rebound.NBodySolver`, `astro.conjunction.propagate_two_body_j2`. Geometry: `pinneapple_twin3d.scene`.

## What already exists in PINNeAPPle (verified)

`solid_fem` (`HexMesh`, `box_mesh`, `SolidFEM`, `von_mises`); `elasticity3d_fdm.solve_static_elasticity_3d`; `nbody_rebound.NBodySolver`; `astro.nbody.integrate`, `energy`, `angular_momentum`; `astro.orbits.elements_from_state`; `astro.conjunction.propagate_two_body_j2`; `pinneapple_twin3d.scene`; `verification.convergence.mesh_independence_study`; `uncertainty.margin.margin_study`; `pinneapple_lab.experiment` with `log_metric`, `check`, `add_file`; `pinneapple_blender.cycles`.

## What is missing (the work)

1. A shell or membrane element with pretension; `solid_fem` is a small-strain hexahedral continuum and cannot represent a thin deployed ring honestly.
2. Deployment kinematics: an ordered fold sequence from the stated envelope to the ring, with inextensibility and a self-collision check.
3. Rotordynamics: spin-up torque, nutation, whirl, and the attitude authority at each ramp step.
4. One optimiser over fold order and ramp rate under both constraints, with a mass estimate including joints and spin-up propellant.
5. The comfort metric computed from the solved ring: gradient and Coriolis trajectory of a thrown object.

## Experiment protocol

Matrix: R x target g in {0.3, 0.6, 1.0} g0 x areal density = 36 structural cases; five ramp rates each = 180 runs. Three mesh levels at ratio 2 for GCI on peak stress. Policy: momentum conserved to 1e-6 per ramp; GCI_fine < 5 %. Budget 200–400 CPU-hours. Logged: stress per step, rate and momentum, nutation, controllability margin, energy, commit.

## Baselines, references and verification

Hoop stress is checked against sigma_a omega^2 R; angular momentum against the analytic rigid-body value. Competitors: a non-rotating ring of the same mass (no gravity, no hoop stress); a rigid ring launched assembled (no deployment risk); a tethered two-mass counterweight, the fair competitor.

## Metrics

Peak hoop stress [MPa] and U [-]. g_app [m/s^2] at the rim and Delta g/g [-]. Coriolis deflection over a 2 m throw [m]. Spin-up energy [GJ]. Nutation damping time [s]. Total mass [t].

## Deliverables

180 records (JSON; npz with per-step stress), under 2 GB. Figures: U versus ramp rate; Delta g/g versus R with the comfort bound drawn. Render of the deployed ring with the gravity field mapped on it.

## Acceptance criteria

- Peak hoop stress agrees with sigma_a omega^2 R within 5 % for a uniform ring.
- Angular momentum conserved to 1e-6 relative across each ramp.
- GCI_fine below 5 % on peak hoop stress at the largest ramp rates.
- Every case names its binding constraint: U reaching 1, or the controllability margin reaching zero.
- A ring that cannot reach the target gravity is reported as such, naming the constraint.

## Realizability and evidence class

PLAUSIBILITY_CLASS today: `established` for centrifugal gravity in a rotating frame; `hypothetical` for a deployed ring of this size. The mechanics is textbook; structure, deployment and mass are not.

Filled: `mathematical_consistency`, `numerical_stability`, `physical_plausibility`. NOT ASSESSED: `experimental_validation`, `technological_feasibility`. `uncertainty_sensitivity` is partly fillable with `margin_study` on areal density and yield.

To move up one class, ranked: a deployable membrane with measured stiffness and pretension retention; a joint that survives the crease cycle and carries hoop load; spin-up propulsion whose mass is small against the ring.

## Risks and failure modes

- A small-strain continuum overstates stiffness once the membrane wrinkles — report compressive membrane stress and void that case.
- Deployment kinematics ignores self-contact — report the collision check per event.
- The optimiser finds a ramp so slow that propellant dominates — charge propellant mass in the objective.
- Comfort thresholds get quoted as if measured — label them as design inputs.

## Out of scope

- Physiological claims about rotating environments.
- Life support, shielding, docking, interior architecture.
