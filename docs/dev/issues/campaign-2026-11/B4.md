## Summary

LEVITATE holds a body in a magnetic field with a closed loop. A magnetostatic solve gives the field gradient, a rigid-body model integrates the force, the plant is linearised about an operating gap, and LQR, MPC and a learned policy compete under noise and coil saturation. The claim: over a stated gap and payload range, the loop holds the body within a position tolerance at a stated power per kilogram. Earnshaw's theorem is asserted, not overcome.

## Question

Over what gap and payload range does a specified closed loop hold the body with position RMS below tolerance, and at what control current and power per kilogram?

## Why run it

It replaces "magnetic levitation" with a stiffness, a current, a bandwidth and a watts-per-kilogram figure, which decides whether a levitating demonstrator is a control problem or a fantasy.

## Physical model

Field by Biot–Savart over filament segments:

B(r) = (mu0 I / 4 pi) ∮ dl x (r - r') / |r - r'|^3

Force as a dipole, F = grad(m . B); dynamics m z'' = F(z, i) - m g + d(t).

Earnshaw sets the frame: in a current-free static region each component of B is harmonic, so no potential minimum exists and at least one stiffness eigenvalue destabilises.

Groups: stiffness per weight k a / (m g); lift margin F_m / (m g); skin depth delta / a; bandwidth ratio omega_c / omega_n.

Left out: coil thermal drift, magnet temperature dependence, flux pinning, real sensors. A hold here is one degree of freedom.

## Geometry and inputs

An axisymmetric coil pair with a separate control winding and a cylindrical permanent-magnet payload of declared remanence, at gaps of 5 to 30 mm. Magnetization, turns and loss coefficients are declared constants, not fitted values.

## Solver and engine

Field: `pinneapple_simulation.electromagnetics.statics` (`biot_savart`, `loop_field`, `finite_solenoid_on_axis_B`). Losses: `electromagnetics.eddy.solve_time_harmonic`, `electromagnetics.hysteresis` (`JilesAtherton`). Body: `particle_dynamics.rigid_body`. Robustness: `pinneapple_analysis.uncertainty.margin.margin_study`, `corner_stackup`.

## What is missing (the work)

1. Two-way coupling between field and position: the modules are static or analytic, so each step re-evaluates or interpolates the field.
2. A state-space control module: linearisation, a Riccati solve for LQR, constrained MPC, a learned policy. The `_riccati` in `electromagnetics/mie.py` is Riccati–Bessel, unrelated.
3. A stability-margin metric reported with the trajectories.
4. Sensor and actuator models: gap-sensor noise, current saturation, coil thermal limit.
5. A power module summing I^2 R, eddy and hysteresis loss per kilogram held.

## Experiment protocol

Operating points: 3 coil geometries x 4 gaps x 3 payload ratios = 36, each with 2 controllers at 3 noise levels, seeds 0 to 2. The step resolves the loop bandwidth; every run repeats at half the step. Ten simulated seconds, a 1 mm step at t = 2 s, a 20 % saturation event at t = 5 s. Budget: order 10^2 CPU-hours.

## Baselines, references and verification

Baselines: open loop, shown unstable with its growth rate; a PID on the linearised model; LQR; an unconstrained actuator as an upper bound; the bias power I0^2 R. Verification: a single loop against `loop_on_axis_B`; stiffness against a finite-difference gradient; the step response against the analytic second-order one.

## Metrics

- k_lev = -dF/dz (N/m), the stiffness the loop must supply.
- I_rms and I_peak (A) against the coil rating.
- p = P_elec / m_held (W/kg) with the loss breakdown.
- t_s (s), settling to 5 % of the final gap.
- z_RMS (mm) per noise level; load range held (kg).
- Gain and phase margin; open-loop unstable eigenvalue (1/s).

## Deliverables

Trajectory files per run (gap, current, power), an operating-point table, margin plots, and a rendered sequence of the body held and disturbed, from the trajectories.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every operating point reports a positive real open-loop eigenvalue; points without one are excluded from the hold claims.
- Over the load range, the better controller holds z_RMS < 0.5 mm and t_s < 1 s.
- I_peak stays inside the coil rating everywhere; saturated runs are failures.
- The loss breakdown sums to the total within 2 %.
- Re-running a case reproduces the trajectory bit-for-bit.

## Realizability and evidence class

Fills `mathematical_consistency`, `numerical_stability`, `physical_plausibility` and `uncertainty_sensitivity`; `experimental_validation` and `technological_feasibility` stay not assessed. `PLAUSIBILITY_CLASS`: `established` for active suspension, `extrapolated` outside the swept ranges.

## Risks and failure modes

1. The controller wins with currents the hardware could not deliver. Mitigation: saturation in the loop; saturated runs fail.
2. Linearisation invalid far from the operating point. Mitigation: linear versus nonlinear steps at the gap extremes.
3. The static field misrepresents the moving case. Mitigation: coupling error against a re-solved field.
4. Thermal drift ignored, so power is optimistic. Mitigation: a derating factor and a sensitivity line.
5. One degree of freedom reported as full levitation. Mitigation: constrained axes named in every caption.

## Out of scope

- Windings, magnets, cryogenics, superconductors, bench hardware; coil thermal hydraulics.
- Six-degree-of-freedom tumbling or vehicle integration.
