## Summary

Up to 20 000 mesoscale units, 0.5 mm to 2 mm (a design choice, not atoms), carry a few binding states and start dispersed. Agitation anneals them into a gear; a command switches the states and the same population re-forms as a gripper. The claim is narrow: a target shape is reached with a measured defect rate, at a computed reconfiguration cost. This models rules on units, not a material.

## Question

With N = 8 000 units and four binding states, does an inverted rule set build the gear with 90 % of units correctly bonded and under 5 % wrong links, then rebuild the gripper after a dissolve command, in 6 of 8 seeds?

## Why run it

Mesoscale self-assembly is established in the laboratory, always as one hand-tuned demonstration with no defect number attached. Missing is the inverse map from target geometry to rule set, and a defect taxonomy with seed variance: missing units, wrong links, porosity, kinetic traps.

## Physical model

Rigid bodies with full orientational freedom, excluded volume from a soft-sphere contact `F_n = k delta`, and bonds that are square wells, `U_b = -eps` for `r < r_b`, broken at an activated rate `nu exp(-eps/kT_eff)`. Agitation is white noise on force and torque at a set `T_eff`, a thermostat standing in for shaking rather than for a flow.

Three numbers fix the regime: `eps/kT_eff`, below about 3 the population melts and above about 12 it freezes into its first trap; site valence `z`, which caps the connectivities the target can express; range ratio `r_b/d`. The inverse problem is bounded by information, `log2(S)/log2(K)` with `K` the distinct local configurations the target needs; below one, no optimizer reaches it. Left out: carrier hydrodynamics, capillarity, screening, and the chemistry that would make a bond real.

## Geometry and inputs

Units are parametric (a cube with six face sites, an octahedron with eight, a rod with two end sites), generated with `pinneapple_design.geometry.gen.primitives` and `sdf_shapes`, and voxelized to a signed distance field. Two targets are authored: a 12 mm spur gear and a two-finger gripper with a 4 mm opening, each voxelized at the unit pitch.

## Solver and engine

No binding engine exists. `pinneapple_simulation.numerical_solvers.particles.DEM` supplies soft-sphere contacts, a Verlet neighbour list and one-way drag, with no bonds, no break process and no per-unit state; `pinneapple_simulation.particle_dynamics.rigid_body` supplies orientation and angular momentum. The mechanical check freezes the bond graph into a joint model and solves it with `SolidFEM`.

## What already exists in PINNeAPPle (verified)

- `pinneapple_simulation.numerical_solvers.particles`: `DEM`, `Liquid`, `drag_factor` — contacts and one-way drag, no binding.
- `pinneapple_simulation.particle_dynamics.rigid_body`: `RigidBody`, `RigidBodySystem`.
- `pinneapple_simulation.numerical_solvers.neighbors`: `build_neighbor_list`.
- `pinneapple_simulation.numerical_solvers.solid_fem`: `SolidFEM`, `box_mesh`, `von_mises`.
- `pinneapple_design.geometry.gen`: `primitives`, `sdf_shapes`.
- `pinneapple_design.bricks`: `analyse_assembly`, `capacity` — joint-network equilibrium for stud bricks; it does not transfer unmodified.
- `pinneapple_lab.experiment`, `pinneapple_futures.plausibility.assess`, `pinneapple_twin3d.scene`.

## What is missing (the work)

1. A binding-rule engine: per-unit site graph and state machine, reversible bonds, conserved valence. New `pinneapple_simulation/particle_dynamics/binding.py`.
2. Bond forces and torques coupled to rigid orientation, seed-driven breaking. New `particle_dynamics/binding_dynamics.py`.
3. Inverse design: voxelize, place, assign site states, extract rules, with the information ceiling computed first. New `pinneapple_design/morphogenesis/inverse.py`.
4. A dissolve-and-reform sequencer. New `pinneapple_design/morphogenesis/sequencing.py`.
5. Defects: correct-bond fraction, wrong-link graph distance, porosity, trap class. New `pinneapple_analysis/assembly/defects.py`.

## Experiment protocol

Matrix: N in {2 000, 8 000, 20 000}; S in {2, 4}; `eps/kT_eff` in {3, 5, 8, 12}; `phi` in {0.10, 0.30}; agitation in {constant, annealed}; both targets. 192 cases, 8 sealed seeds. Per seed: disperse, agitate to a work budget, freeze the bond graph, dissolve, agitate again, freeze. A seed whose quiescent energy drifts over 2 % is rejected and reported. One case repeats at 4x N. Budget: about 1 500 CPU-hours.

## Baselines, references and verification

An isolated bond pair must show a bonded fraction matching the two-state Boltzmann factor `1/(1+exp(eps/kT_eff))` within 2 %. Assembled stiffness is compared with the same geometry as a continuum in `SolidFEM`; a discrepancy beyond a factor of two is reported, not omitted. Baselines: unagitated deposition; agitation with no rule set; a hand-designed gear rule set; reassembly from scratch.

## Metrics

`eta_c` correct bonds over target bonds; `eta_w` bonds outside the target graph over all bonds; IoU of occupied unit voxels with the target voxel set; `p` porosity on the unit grid; `t_a` agitation work to the stop criterion; `E_r` work per reconfiguration; yield as passing seeds out of 8 with a Wilson interval. All dimensionless.

## Deliverables

`manifest.json`, `metrics.csv` (192 cases x 8 seeds), per-seed bond graphs, full configurations for 12 cases as `.npz`, coverage and defect figures, a 30 s gear-to-gripper video through `pinneapple_twin3d.scene`, and a report naming what the information ceiling leaves unreachable.

## Acceptance criteria

- The bond-fraction check passes within 2 % before any assembly number is reported.
- At N = 8 000, S = 4, `eta_c` exceeds 0.90 and `eta_w` stays under 0.05 in at least 6 of 8 seeds.
- Coverage carries a 4x-N refinement gap under 5 %.
- Gripper IoU exceeds 0.80 in at least 4 of 8 seeds, with the trap class reported per failure.
- Stiffness agrees with the `SolidFEM` continuum within a factor of two, or the ratio is stated.

## Realizability and evidence class

Filled: mathematical_consistency (bond statistics against the Boltzmann factor, the `SolidFEM` cross-check); numerical_stability (4x unit count, energy-drift rejection); physical_plausibility (excluded volume, detailed-balance breaking, the information ceiling). Partially filled: uncertainty_sensitivity, since seeds give a binomial rate but `eps`, `r_b` and `z` are held fixed within a case.

Not assessed: experimental_validation (no unit was built) and technological_feasibility beyond a scale argument. A 1 mm unit with a magnetic binding state is reachable by existing fabrication, but nothing here speaks to a manufacturable material, and nothing to nanomaterials.

PLAUSIBILITY_CLASS today: hypothetical. Mesoscale self-assembly is established; reversible construction of different parts from one population under a command is not. Moving to extrapolated needs the inverse map to hold on a bench rig at a comparable defect rate.

## Risks and failure modes

1. The optimizer fits the exact target voxelization. Mitigation: a held-out third target reported as a failure.
2. Kinetic traps read as a physics limit when they are a schedule artifact. Mitigation: agitation is an axis, never pooled.
3. A miscomputed information ceiling can excuse every failure. Mitigation: check it by brute force on a small instance.

## Out of scope

- Any nanoscale or molecular claim; the units are design choices, not atoms.
- Bond chemistry, surface functionalization and unit fabrication.
- Closed-loop control.
