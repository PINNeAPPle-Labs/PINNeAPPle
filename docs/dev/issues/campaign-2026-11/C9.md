## Summary

Components of order 1-100 µm suspended in a channel are carried by a creeping flow past a designed obstacle landscape and bind at stations into a small assembly. The run reports yield — assemblies completed per unit time, component waste fraction, blockage rate — per layout and flow rate. The components are point particles with prescribed binding rules, so the claim is about a transport scheme at that scale, not about a manufacturing process.

## Question

For a channel of fixed footprint at Re < 0.1, is the yield of correct assemblies above 50 percent at a waste fraction below 20 percent, and which layouts fail outright?

## Why run it

At low Reynolds number the flow is reversible and particles follow streamlines, so assembly must come from geometry — obstacle arrays that shift streamlines and force encounters — not from inertia. Whether geometry alone yields enough is falsifiable and cheap to sweep in 2-D. The engineering value is a yield map with a stated waste fraction.

## Physical model

Stokes flow, mu lap(u) = grad(p) and div(u) = 0. Particles: m dv/dt = 6 pi mu a (u_f - v) + F_b, with F_b set by the fluctuation-dissipation relation at T. Binding is Poisson on contact with rate k_on, release with k_off; those constants decide whether assembly is possible at all.

Deciding groups: Re = rho U L / mu; Pe = U L / D with D = k T / (6 pi mu a); blockage ratio d/H; Da = k_on over the encounter rate. Valid for continuum flow, point particles, no inertia; absent are contact mechanics, colloidal forces beyond a hard core and any chemistry making a binding site selective.

## Geometry and inputs

A 2-D channel of 20 H by 5 H whose obstacle array is an SDF mask from `pinneapple_design.geometry.gen`; pitch, radius and row offset are the design variables, with three stations of fixed footprint and a release gate. The 3-D check extrudes the best layout through `pinneapple_simulation.pipelines.freecad_openfoam` (`PipelineSpec`, `run_pipeline`, `Sweep`, `doe`).

## Solver and engine

In-process `numerical_solvers.lbm` (`LBMSolver`, `cylinder_mask`, `rectangle_mask`) at low Mach for the 2-D sweeps, with body-fitted confirmation through `external_solvers.openfoam`. `pinneapple_design.topology.StokesDissipation2D` optimizes a Stokes objective, not transport, so it is only a layout seed.

## What already exists in PINNeAPPle (verified)

- `numerical_solvers.lbm`: `LBMSolver`, `cylinder_mask`, `rectangle_mask`, D2Q9 and D3Q19 paths, bounce-back walls.
- `numerical_solvers.internal_flow`: `Route`, `InternalFlow`, `colebrook` — pipe-scale friction and mixing, not creeping particle transport.
- `particles`: `DEM`, `drag_factor`, `terminal_velocity`; `neighbors.build_neighbor_list`.
- `pipelines.freecad_openfoam`: `PipelineSpec`, `run_pipeline`, `Sweep`, `doe`, `fit_surrogate`.
- `pinneapple_lab.experiment`, `pinneapple_analysis.verification.convergence`.

## What is missing (the work)

1. A creeping-flow channel solver stable at Re < 0.1, or a documented policy for reaching that regime with LBM — `pinneapple_simulation/microscale/stokes_channel.py`.
2. Lagrangian transport with one-way coupling, Brownian kicks and a reflecting hard core — `pinneapple_simulation/microscale/transport.py`.
3. A per-component binding and release state machine with rates, not a geometric pass/fail.
4. Yield accounting: assemblies, waste, blockages, per-station capture.
5. A layout search against yield rather than dissipation.

## Experiment protocol

4 layouts (straight, staggered, chevron, hand-designed) x 4 Pe x 3 k_on x 2 inlets = 96 cases, 5 seeds each, 2000 components per seed, 20 flow-through times per run. Two refinements per layout with `convergence`, target GCI below 5 percent. Budget: order 10^2 CPU-hours, order 10^3 with the 3-D confirmation.

## Baselines, references and verification

Analytic Poiseuille to within 2 percent; Stokes drag and the Brownian 2Dt reproduced before binding is enabled; particle count conserved exactly. At Re << 1 a particle returns to its starting streamline on flow reversal, the check that the regime is right. Baselines: straight channel, random layout at equal solid fraction, hand-designed layout.

## Metrics

- Y = assemblies per hour; W = wasted over injected, percent.
- B = blockage episodes per hour and cross-section fraction lost at peak.
- eta_s = capture efficiency per station, percent; tau = mean injection-to-assembly time.
- dY/dPe over the sampled range.

## Deliverables

Per-case manifest, compressed trajectory archive (order 10^8 bytes), yield table with confidence intervals, yield-versus-Pe map per layout, station capture figures, one cycle video from `pinneapple_twin3d.scene`, and the zero-yield layout list.

## Acceptance criteria

- Analytic Poiseuille and Brownian 2Dt within 2 percent before any assembly run.
- Component count conserved to machine precision; none leaves the domain.
- GCI below 5 percent on centreline velocity and capture efficiency.
- Every yield carries a seed-based confidence interval, zero-yield layouts included.
- A case re-run from its manifest reproduces Y within its interval.

## Realizability and evidence class

Fills `mathematical_consistency`, `numerical_stability`, `physical_plausibility`, and `uncertainty_sensitivity` partially through the k_on sweep. Leaves `experimental_validation` and `technological_feasibility` not assessed: no component was fabricated. `PLAUSIBILITY_CLASS` today: `extrapolated`; `established` needs measured binding rates and an experimental yield from the same layout.

## Risks and failure modes

1. LBM at Re < 0.1 is slow and drift-prone. Mitigation: a Stokes alternative, steady-state check per case.
2. An arbitrary k_on sets the yield. Mitigation: report a surface over k_on.
3. "Assembly" is a rule, not chemistry. Mitigation: state the rule in each caption.
4. Blockage is rare and underestimated. Mitigation: enough seeds to bound it.
5. The cycle video becomes the result. Mitigation: yield first, video last.

## Out of scope

- Fabrication, surface chemistry, electrokinetics, colloidal force models.
- Inertial focusing and viscoelastic migration, which need Re far above this range.
- Claims about a manufacturable nanomaterial or a production line.
