Build instructions

  1. Make the bracket from Aluminium 6061-T6: 17.0 mm plate, 36.0 mm wide; mounting leg 72 mm, arm 100 mm (bracket.step).
  2. Inner corner fillet R17.0 mm (bend radius if formed from sheet; machined otherwise). A sharp inner corner raises the peak stress above the verified value.
  3. Two 6.6 mm clearance holes for M6 bolts in the mounting leg, on its centre line between the fillet and the end (positions in bracket.step).
  4. Bolt to a rigid, flat support with two M6 8.8 bolts and washers.
  5. Apply the load at the end of the arm, perpendicular to it, as assumed in the design.

Limits and assumptions

  • Static load only: no fatigue, impact, vibration or creep.
  • The mounting face is idealised as fully clamped; a flexible wall or anchor changes the stresses and is not checked (nor is the anchor's pull-out).
  • Bolt check: tension from the moment about the toe; prying and shear are not included.
  • Linear elastic: valid while the peak stays below yield (the safety factor keeps it there).
  • the load acts at the end of the arm, perpendicular to it (a shelf load)
  • the mounting leg's face is fully clamped by its two M6 bolts (the support is rigid)
  • inner-corner fillet radius equal to the thickness
  • static load; no fatigue, impact or temperature effects
  • safety_factor = 2 (template default (engineering convention for a known static load; state yours))
