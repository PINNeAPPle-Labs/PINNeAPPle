Physics plan

  • steady, incompressible, attached flow around the wing
  • lift = weight in steady glide; induced drag from the span loading (vortex lattice / lifting line)
  • profile drag from turbulent flat-plate friction and a thickness form factor
  • sink rate = V C_D / C_L

Build instructions

  1. Wing: span 200 cm, root chord 39.2 cm, tip chord 12.8 cm (straight taper), section NACA 2412, no twist.
  2. Size the spar for the launch loads separately: no structural check is part of this design.
  3. Balance and trim the glider to fly at about 7.2 m/s.

Limits and assumptions

  • Wing only: fuselage, tail and interference drag are not included.
  • Inviscid lift (vortex lattice): no stall prediction; the design lift coefficient is capped instead.
  • Profile drag from a flat-plate correlation, not from a viscous solver.
  • No structural sizing (spar, launch loads).
  • wing alone: the fuselage and tail drag are not included (the sink rate is the wing's share)
  • air density 1.225 kg/m^3 (ICAO standard atmosphere, sea level)
  • fully turbulent skin friction (conservative for the profile drag at model Reynolds numbers)
  • no twist, no sweep, no dihedral in the aerodynamic model
  • cl_limit = 0.8 (template design limit kept below stall; stall is not predicted by these models)
