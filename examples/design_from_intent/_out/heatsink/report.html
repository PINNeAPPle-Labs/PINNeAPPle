Build instructions

  1. Extrude or machine the heat sink in Copper C110: base 100 x 100 x 6.5 mm with 14 fins 1.1 mm thick and 47 mm tall (heatsink.step).
  2. Centre the heat source on the base; apply the thermal paste thinly over the whole footprint.
  3. Mount with the fins vertical and nothing blocking the air below and above them.
  4. Check: at full power the case temperature should settle below the limit; the operating window gives the margin.

Limits and assumptions

  • Correlation-based convection: +-15 % on h is the stated band of the correlations, not a measured uncertainty of this part.
  • Radiation is neglected (conservative).
  • The full model resolves conduction in the base; the fins and the air are modelled by the correlations, not by CFD.
  • The source temperature is the case / lid temperature; the junction is hotter by the package's own resistance (not modelled).
  • vertical fins with air rising along the base depth (natural convection)
  • radiation neglected (conservative: real parts run cooler, more so if anodised)
  • a fan's air is ducted between the fins (forced convection)
  • air at 1 atm; properties from Incropera & DeWitt Table A.4
  • t_ambient = 298.1 K (template default: room temperature 25 °C (state yours))
  • source_size = 0.03 m (pinneapple_design.thermal.heatsink sizer default (30 x 30 mm): affects the spreading resistance; state yours)
  • tim = 1e-05 K*m^2/W (pinneapple_design.thermal.heatsink sizer default (thermal grease, 10 K·mm²/W))
  • max_width = 0.1 m (pinneapple_design.thermal.heatsink sizer default envelope (100 mm))
  • max_depth = 0.1 m (pinneapple_design.thermal.heatsink sizer default envelope (100 mm))
  • max_height = 0.05 m (pinneapple_design.thermal.heatsink sizer default envelope (50 mm))
