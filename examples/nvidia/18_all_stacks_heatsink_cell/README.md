# 18 Heat-sink work cell with every stack: PINNeAPPle + B + P + N + I + C + O

**What.** One small engineering task through all six stacks, with PINNeAPPle checking every hand-off:

1. **B, NVIDIA USD BRep:** exact solids of 110 sampled plate-fin heat sinks (fin count, height, thickness, root
   fillets) and their exact mass.
2. **P, PhysicsNeMo:** `FullyConnected` surrogate `(n, H, t) -> (T rise, dp, mass)`, trained on the B-rep masses and
   PINNeAPPle's closed-form thermal rating (60 W, 3 m/s air).
3. **N, PINNeAPPle Model Services:** the surrogate behind the NIM-shaped `/v1/infer`, with a trust report per design.
   The client asks it about 9 765 designs and takes the lightest one meeting T <= 75 C and dp <= 30 Pa among those
   the service trusts. PINNeAPPle verifies that proposal with the exact solid and the closed form.
4. **I, PINNeAPPle Robot Sim on NVIDIA Newton:** the two-link arm carries the verified part plus a 2 kg gripper.
   Seven move durations are tried; each must pass replay convergence, tracking, energy balance, motor limits and
   link stress.
5. **C, PINNeAPPle World Model:** synthetic side-camera frames of the chosen move (4 camera variations), with the
   check that the part in the pixels is where the kinematics put it.
6. **O, OpenUSD:** the move with the exact part mesh, the camera and the decisions in one validated stage
   (`results/cell.usda`, regenerated by each run, not committed).

| Step | Who | Ran here |
|---|---|---|
| exact solids, mass (90 training + 20 test designs) | NVIDIA USD BRep 1.0.1 | yes |
| closed-form rating, verification | PINNeAPPle | yes |
| FullyConnected 3 x 64, 12 000 Adam steps | PhysicsNeMo 2.2.2 (CPU) | yes |
| `/v1/infer` over local HTTP, trust report | PINNeAPPle Model Services | yes |
| arm moves with the part | Robot Sim (Newton 1.7) | yes |
| synthetic camera frames, pixel vs kinematics check | PINNeAPPle World Model | yes |
| cell stage, validation | OpenUSD (`usd-core` 26.11) | yes |
| RTX render (`results/kit_render.py`), Isaac Sim, NVIDIA NIM, Cosmos | Omniverse / NVIDIA | no (GPU or `NVIDIA_API_KEY`) |

## Results (this run: 4-core Xeon 2.1 GHz, no GPU)

- Surrogate held-out error (relative RMS, 20 designs): T rise 0.33 %, dp 2.7 %, mass 0.09 %.
- The service flagged 1 500 of the 9 765 queried designs as outside its training box. They were not considered.
  One query took 19 ms of inference and 102 ms of trust checks.
- First proposal accepted: **13 fins, 17 mm high, 1.1 mm thick**.
  - Served 73.76 C; verified **73.99 C**, 8.1 Pa, **200.6 g** (exact B-rep mass).
- Arm with 2.20 kg payload:
  - 0.8, 0.6 and 0.5 s moves pass. The 0.5 s move peaks the shoulder at 112.5 of 120 N m.
  - At 0.4 s and below the shoulder saturates at 120 N m, so tracking and replay fail.
  - **Shortest passing move: 0.5 s.**
- The part in the camera frames is at the kinematic tool point within **0.95 px** (limit 2 px), over 4 camera
  variations.
- The cell USD stage validates: 4 meshes, 144 time samples.
- **Decision: APPROVED.**
