    Digital Twin Library: eleven systems, each with its own conservation laws¶

    pinneapple_systems.digital_twin.library holds reference models of eleven engineering systems — an FPSO with its subsea well, a blast furnace, a chemical synthesis loop, a reusable booster, a lunar base, a gas pipeline network, a compression station, a green hydrogen plant, a seawater desalination train, an industrial refrigeration plant and a data centre. They are the bottom rung of the fidelity ladder the Digital Twin Library issues describe: cheap enough to run in a test suite, explicit about their assumptions, and the source of the solver data a trained surrogate would later be fitted to.

    Every one of them takes SI parameters with declared ranges, returns a machine-readable result with its conservation balances, its invariants, its assumptions, its limits and what it did not verify, and states where every external number came from.

    from pinneapple_systems.digital_twin.library import catalogue, run, describe, compare

print(compare())            # every system on its declared default, with its verdict
result = run("fpso_subsea") # one system, default scenario
print(result.report())      # outputs, balances, invariants, limits, verdict
print(describe("fpso_subsea"))   # parameters with ranges, assumptions, sources
result.save("fpso.json")    # the whole run as JSON, provenance included

    Why balances and not just outputs¶

    A model that only prints numbers cannot be wrong on its own terms. Each system here computes its conservation laws twice, by different routes, and reports the residual:

    System Balances The two routes
    fpso_subsea oil, gas, lift, energy tank pressure decline against the integrated oil rate; endpoint enthalpy against the integrated heat flux along the line
    blast_furnace iron, carbon, oxygen, mass, raceway energy iron charged against iron tapped and lost; total mass from molar masses, independent of the stoichiometry algebra
    chemical_plant A atoms, B atoms, energy make-up against product plus purge, at the steady state the loop solves for
    reusable_booster mass, momentum, energy acceleration integrated along the path against the endpoint velocity; specific power against kinetic plus potential energy
    lunar_base energy, oxygen, water generation against day load plus storage; regolith yield against crew consumption
    pipeline_network mass, energy, linepack the stations' polytropic shaft work against cp times the temperature rise it produces; the closed-form linepack integral against a 200-node quadrature of the pressure profile
    compression_station mass, energy, cooling the shaft work from cp ΔT against the integral of v dp along the polytropic path, stage by stage; the intercoolers against the enthalpy the previous stage added
    green_hydrogen_plant electricity, energy, water, oxygen the power asked for against cells × voltage × current; hydrogen at its heating value plus the heat the overpotential rejects against the electricity in; the water the stoichiometry demands against the mass of the two gases it became
    desalination_plant water, salt, energy salt in the feed against salt in permeate plus concentrate, with the concentrate's concentration derived from that balance and then used to set the osmotic pressure; the pump less the energy-recovery device against the specific energy times the permeate flow
    industrial_refrigeration energy, duty the compressor's work as the isentropic enthalpy rise against the integral of v dp along the same path; the first law round the loop against the condenser's own enthalpy route
    data_center_cooling energy the facility built up component by component against the heat the cooling plant must move. In a data centre every watt leaves as heat, so this is a completeness check and the one that fails when a PUE is wrong

    A residual above the declared tolerance fails the run, and result.ok is False. run.require() raises BalanceFailed with every failing line named.

    The invariants are separate: they are things that must hold for any parameter set — the well produces nothing at zero drawdown, the booster cannot beat its own Tsiolkovsky limit, the lunar night is half a synodic month, a wet blast runs the raceway colder.

    Coupling the systems the way the physics couples them¶

    The interesting part of each model is what it solves rather than computes:

    • the FPSO finds its operating point where the reservoir's inflow curve crosses the line's outflow curve, by bisection on the bottom-hole pressure;
    • the blast furnace's top-gas composition and degree of direct reduction fall out of the carbon and oxygen balances together;
    • the chemical loop's recycle composition is solved in closed form, so the steady state is exact rather than the limit of a slow iteration;
    • the booster's boostback burn is solved so the stage lands on its own pad — the burn is commanded in velocity, and the last step of the burn is trimmed to it, because a burn that always ends on a whole time step quantises downrange to hundreds of metres;
    • the lunar base is sized by its night, and the night comes from the synodic month;
    • the pipeline's throughput is the flow at which the pressure the line needs at its inlet is exactly the pressure the supply offers — the stations are set to a ratio and the line takes what it can;
    • the compression station proves the equal-ratio split instead of asserting it: it searches the split and checks that no other division of the same total ratio costs less work;
    • the electrolyser's hydrogen is what Faraday's law says it is, and its heat is what the cell voltage says is left over — the two share no algebra, so the energy balance can fail;
    • the desalination train derives its brine concentration from the salt balance and then uses it to set the osmotic pressure the pump has to beat, and every run is checked against the reversible separation work computed as an integral rather than quoted;
    • the refrigeration cycle is built from correlations that can each be checked against published data — Antoine reproduces ammonia's normal boiling point, Watson is anchored on its latent heat there — and the condenser balance reports the property model's own inconsistency (a few per cent) instead of hiding it;
    • the data centre states its own limitation: in a hall where every watt leaves as heat the first law is a tautology, so the single balance is a completeness check on the PUE roll-up rather than two dressed-up copies of the same sum.

    Named scenarios and the registry's status¶

    A parameter set that says something is worth keeping, so it has a name:

    from pinneapple_systems.digital_twin.library import SCENARIOS, run_scenario, manifest, save_scenarios

run_scenario("pipeline_network/low_delivery_pressure")   # a case, not a keyword soup
manifest()["summary"]                                    # every twin and every case, with verdicts
save_scenarios("my_cases.json")                          # hand a case to somebody else

    Each scenario is a slug, a name, a few overrides and a line saying where the numbers came from. It runs through the same simulate as anything else, so a scenario can never bypass a range check.

    Four of the eleven shipped scenarios trip an invariant on purpose, and they are the most useful ones: a 3:1 ratio in a single compression stage discharges at 143 °C, above the machine limit; a 69 K lift in a single-stage ammonia machine discharges above 150 °C, which is where a two-stage machine starts; a heavier booster runs out of landing propellant; and a cold-climate data hall has an economiser that costs more than the chiller it replaces, because the fixed free-cooling share is not sized against the plant. They carry expect="finding" instead of being tuned until they pass, and the check is strict in both directions — a finding that stops being reported is a regression too.

    python -m pinneapple_systems.digital_twin.library.scenarios --check is the CI hook: it exits 1 if a case that had to pass failed, and prints what the library's own status page says.

    Ranges, extrapolation and honesty¶

    A parameter outside its declared range raises ParameterError unless the caller asks for it:

    model = build("lunar_base")
model.simulate(crew=40)                 # ParameterError: outside [1, 12]
model.simulate(crew=40, strict=False)   # runs, and records the extrapolation in the result
result.extrapolated                     # ['crew = 40.0 - outside [1.0, 12.0]']

    The range is the range in which the model's equations are the right physics, not where the arithmetic survives: the hydrostatic line is valid at 50 bar and arithmetic at 5 000 bar.

    The ladder¶

    Each entry in the catalogue carries its fidelity and the next rung:

    for entry in catalogue():
    print(entry.slug, entry.fidelity, entry.status)

    The 0D/1D models here are the first rung of the Digital Twin Library issues (#1770, #1772, #1774, #1777, #1779, #1784, #1801, #1813, #1814, #1807, #1819). The registry and the scenario manifest are the discoverability half of the shared-specification issue (#1802); the typed unit algebra, the component graph with ports, the solver adapter protocol, the cross-fidelity consistency checks and the VeriPhysics applicability gate are not here, and that issue is not closed by them. What comes above them — 1D transients, 2D/3D solvers, trained surrogates with an OOD domain, VeriPhysics gates, the Engineering API workers and the Studio scenes — is tracked in those issues, and none of it is claimed here.

    Tests¶

    tests/test_digital_twin_library.py runs every system and checks, for each one, that its balances close and its invariants hold, that a run is reproducible, and — separately — that the model reproduces answers computed by hand in the test: Vogel's published curve, a straight-line productivity index as its zero-exponent special case, LMTD for a line with no Joule-Thomson cooling, the raceway balance from published enthalpies, Gibbs energy for the equilibrium constant, van't Hoff on the function actually used, the exponential atmosphere's scale height, Stefan-Boltzmann for the radiator and the FeO oxygen yield, the isothermal line equation written out factor by factor, the linepack integral collapsed both to p V / Z R T at a flat pressure and to the mean of a parabolic pressure profile, the polytropic stage work for one stage and the hand sum for three, and Colebrook-White solved for the friction factor the run reports, Faraday's law and the two water voltages, the nine kilograms of water per kilogram of hydrogen, van't Hoff's thirty bar for seawater, the separation-work integral against a quadrature of the same integrand, Antoine's normal boiling point and its inverse, Watson anchored at the boiling point, and the data centre's air temperature rise and PUE recomputed from their own definitions.
