    A surrogate conditioned on the geometry, and the error on a body it never saw¶

    Roadmap item G11 (#83) asks for one thing: a case with a geometry outside the training set, and the error reported on it. This page is that case, with the number.

    The published number: relative L2 of the pressure-coefficient field on the new bodies, 12.0 % (mean over three training seeds, 11.5 % to 12.8 %), worst body 17.2 %. On bodies inside the training box but still unseen it is 8.7 % (8.2 % to 9.4 %), and on the bodies it trained on 6.9 % (6.4 % to 7.6 %). The same model keeps the ranking of the designs by lift: flip rate 0.7 % of the design pairs, Spearman 0.996, no change of the chosen design in any seed.

    python -m benchmarks.geometry_conditioned_surrogate          # the published protocol, about 5 minutes on one CPU
python -m benchmarks.geometry_conditioned_surrogate --quick  # the test budget, under a minute, not a benchmark

    The result file is benchmarks/_out/geometry_conditioned_surrogate.json and the labelled design table is next to it (..._designs.csv). The gate that keeps this page honest is tests/test_geometry_conditioned_surrogate.py: it re-runs the panel frame check (a predicted field integrated with the solver's own pressure integration must give the solver's lift), the split (a "new geometry" has a weight outside the training box by construction), the report, and the protocol of the committed result.

    What "conditioned on the geometry" means here¶

    The surrogate never receives the six numbers that define the body. It receives the geometry: the panel mid-points of the body, one outward normal per panel, and a geometric code pooled from them by the DeepSets encoder of pinneapple_neural.lpm, with multi-scale neighbourhoods at each query point. That is the difference between a parameter study and a geometry-conditioned surrogate: the shape reaches the model as a point set, so the same model can answer for a body that arrives as a mesh, an STL or a drawing, and the six CST weights are only how this page happens to generate the family.

    The family and the labels¶

    • geometry — closed 2-D bodies of the CST (Kulfan) basis the library's airfoil work uses: thickness weights T0..T2 and camber weights M0..M2, from pinneapple_design.aero.geometry with its documented bounds;
    • label — the pressure coefficient Cp on every panel, from the Hess-Smith panel method (pinneapple_design.aero.panel.panel_solve): the inviscid potential-flow reference, which is what any potential-flow or neural model can at best reach. 100 panels per body, and the whole design set is labelled in under a tenth of a second;
    • the split — training bodies are a Latin hypercube inside the training box: the documented parameter ranges pulled back by 20 % at each end. Held out are bodies inside that box (interpolation), new geometries drawn in the outer band so that at least one weight is outside everything the model was trained on, and two named real airfoils fitted to the same basis: NACA 2412 (inside the box) and NACA 4412, whose M1/M2 fall outside both the training box and the documented bounds of that weight.
    setting published protocol
    bodies trained on 24
    bodies held out inside the box 4
    new geometries 4 drawn in the outer band + NACA 2412 + NACA 4412
    panels per body 100 (50 per side, blunt trailing edge closed)
    training 1500 epochs, Adam with cosine decay, lr 2e-3, ensemble of 3 bagged heads, width 96, code 32
    seeds 7, 8, 9 (same design set, three trainings)
    angle of attack 0 deg

    The measured error¶

    body group outside the training box relative L2 Cp min (solver -> surrogate) Cl (solver -> surrogate) OOD
    cst00 unseen - 0.0939 -0.529 -> -0.548 0.125 -> 0.152 0.39
    cst01 unseen - 0.0735 -0.606 -> -0.610 0.313 -> 0.299 0.30
    cst02 unseen - 0.1267 -0.559 -> -0.506 0.198 -> 0.175 0.92
    cst03 unseen - 0.0529 -0.505 -> -0.530 0.116 -> 0.126 0.34
    cst00 new T0, T2, M0, M1 0.1339 -0.838 -> -0.731 0.208 -> 0.226 0.53
    cst01 new T1 0.0909 -0.441 -> -0.432 0.116 -> 0.103 0.53
    cst02 new T1, T2, M1, M2 0.1466 -0.724 -> -0.661 0.369 -> 0.314 1.17
    cst03 new T0, M2 0.1388 -0.368 -> -0.348 -0.026 -> -0.026 0.37
    NACA 2412 new - 0.0388 -0.555 -> -0.580 0.243 -> 0.252 0.37
    NACA 4412 new M1, M2 0.1716 -0.731 -> -0.720 0.485 -> 0.539 0.91

    relative L2 is the mean over the three seeds; Cp min is the suction peak, the number that sets the lift; Cl is integrated from the predicted field with the solver's own pressure integration, so coefficient and field never disagree; OOD is the library's own geometry-code distance to the training set, relative to the usual distance between training designs (the model's own warning, not the error).

    Three things the table says plainly:

    • the error grows as the body gets further from the training set -- 6.9 % on the trained bodies, 8.7 % on unseen bodies inside the box, 12.0 % on bodies in the outer band -- and the worst body is the one that is outside the documented bounds of the family (NACA 4412, 17.2 %);
    • a real airfoil that happens to sit inside the trained box (NACA 2412) is the easiest body of the held-out set (3.9 %), which is the useful direction: the model does not care that the weights were written by NACA or by a sampler;
    • the model's own OOD score does not track the error (the worst body scores 0.91, the hardest in-box body 0.92): a geometry distance is a warning about the design space, not a prediction of accuracy.

    The decision level¶

    A field error does not say whether the surrogate changes the design. Over the 10 held-out bodies, asking "which body carries the most lift" (pinneapple_analysis.decision):

    metric value
    flip rate (pairs of designs whose order is reversed) 0.7 %
    Spearman rho / Kendall tau-b 0.996 / 0.985
    top-k kept (k = 1) 100 %
    regret (the design the surrogate picks, against the best) 0
    the chosen design changed in none of the three seeds

    What this case is not¶

    • The reference is inviscid. The model learns the panel method's Cp, not a viscous flow: the absolute error levels are not comparable with a RANS benchmark, and no claim is made that they are.
    • One family. CST sections at 0 deg, one chord, no compressibility, no viscous decambering. Generalisation is measured inside the family and its outer band, not onto an arbitrary shape.
    • The 2-D bodies come from pinneapple_design.aero.geometry, not from pinneapple_design.geometry_library. The library's entries are 3-D assemblies whose solid kernel is manifold3d (not installed in this environment), and labelling a 3-D body needs a field solver that costs minutes per design, which would make the case a campaign rather than a case. The step that would connect the library to this model is a section of a library body used as the body's point set; the surrogate itself needs nothing else.
    • Two digits are stable, three are not. The spread over seeds is reported next to every mean for that reason.
