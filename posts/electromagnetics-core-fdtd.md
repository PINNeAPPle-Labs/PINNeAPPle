An electromagnetic solver that "looks right" is not enough. A scattered field can be smooth, symmetric and still a few percent off, and you only find out against a reference.

What is in PINNeAPPle now (`pp.em`, examples/electrodynamics/01 to 08):
- statics and closed forms: capacitors, Biot-Savart, inductance, Fresnel, multilayers, waveguide and cavity modes, dipoles, Mie series;
- media: Drude, Debye and Lorentz poles, anisotropic and nonlinear magnetic materials;
- 2-D FEM for electrostatics, magnetostatics and eddy currents;
- FDTD on the Yee grid in 1-D, 2-D and 3-D, with CPML, TF/SF plane waves, dispersive media and a near-to-far-field transform;
- `pp.maxwell.maxwell_checks`: Gauss, Faraday, Ampere-Maxwell and Poynting residuals of any field, from a solver or a neural network. The same checks run in CI through `pinneapple check` with `physics = "maxwell"`.

Measured, from the committed verification run:
- `pp.em.verification.run()`: 32 cases, all pass. Mie efficiencies within 1.4e-6 of Bohren & Huffman's published values; FDTD waveguide TE10 propagation constant within 3.9e-5 of the closed form.
- FDTD cylinder scattering converges at second order against the series solution: 3.8e-2 down to 4.2e-3 from 20 to 60 cells per wavelength.
- The FDTD snapshots pass the Maxwell checks without a reference: Poynting balance 1.6e-4, decision APPROVED.

Limits are stated, not hidden: on a 3-D sphere the FDTD error stalls near 2 % against Mie (2.1e-2, 1.8e-2, 2.4e-2 at 10, 15, 20 cells per wavelength), because the sub-cell averaging is not full anisotropic smoothing. The table in the guide is regenerated by the script.

Guide and code: https://github.com/PINNeAPPle-Labs/PINNeAPPle/blob/main/docs/guides/electromagnetics.md
