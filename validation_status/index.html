    Validation status¶

    Generated by scripts/build_method_status.py --public from pinneapple_catalog/methods.py and pinneapple_catalog/method_status.json (test scan of 2026-10-09). Do not edit by hand.

    Rule. An item is validated when at least one test function that exercises it compares the result with an independent reference (closed-form or manufactured solution, published or tabulated value). Tested means it has tests, none of which compares with a reference. Only validated items are in pinneapple_catalog.validated_methods(). Every status lists its evidence in method_status.json.
